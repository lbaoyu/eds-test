Brand governance and permissions

Protect brand integrity and manage access

Governance capabilities can help teams operationalize brand policies and understand permissions, but availability and the ability to take action depend on the workflow.

GOVERNANCE · 01GA capabilities · access varies

Governance Agent

Part of the GA AEM agentic capabilities in CX Enterprise Coworker. It can check content and assets against brand rules such as tone, claims, logo usage, typography, and imagery. Teams can import brand policies and turn guidance into structured checks.

Governance Agent overviewImport a brand policy
GOVERNANCE · 02Documented · capability-dependent

Permission and digital-rights workflows

In Content Hub, the Governance Agent can help manage attribute-based permissions and usage rights. In AEM, it can explain effective permissions and identify policies behind access decisions. The AEM permission-audit function is read-only: it diagnoses and recommends but does not change ACLs. Governance through the MCP workflow requires an Agents trial or paid license.

Before a customer walkthrough

Validate availability in context

“Released” or “documented” does not mean a capability is enabled in every customer environment. Confirm the customer's release, configuration, and access—especially where public documentation does not explicitly state GA, Limited Availability, or Beta.

CHECK · 01Release

Confirm the customer's AEM release and any minimum-release requirement.

CHECK · 02Configuration

Verify required services, integrations, editors, and environment setup.

CHECK · 03Access and terms

Confirm permissions, program enrollment, eligibility, and licensing.

Scope note: This overview covers Sites, Assets, Content Hub, Dynamic Media, Cloud Manager, and platform operations. Forms and Edge Delivery-specific items are excluded. The latest published release notes referenced here are for AEM as a Cloud Service 2026.9.0.
