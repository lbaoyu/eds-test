The opportunity

From visibility score to operating process

Sixty days to understand where your brand stands in AI-powered discovery, identify where the market says to act, ship one meaningful improvement, re-test the same customer questions, and leave with a repeatable operating model and a clear next step.

A visibility score is only the starting point. This pilot turns AI visibility into an operating process: understand the market, define the customer questions that matter, diagnose why a gap exists, connect it to an owned change, re-test the same questions, and measure what changed — with Adobe alongside your team.

Pilot principle

Measure → Understand → Act → Validate → Learn. The same customer question that reveals an opportunity stays connected to the diagnosis, action, owner, deployment, re-test, and measured result.

Powered by Adobe Brand Visibility — Semrush-powered market and competitive intelligence feeds the GEO engine built by Adobe LLM Optimizer, connecting prompt strategy, brand presence and claims, optimization, collaboration, deployment, and impact measurement in one workflow. Exact capabilities depend on your entitlement and are confirmed with you during Frame.

Activate & Measure

One complete GEO operating loop

The proof engine. One complete loop is required: the customer question that exposes a gap stays connected to the diagnosis, prioritized action, owner, deployment, re-test, and measured outcome.

01Question
02Observe
03Diagnose
04Prioritize & assign
05Optimize & deploy
06Re-test
07Measure
08Learn & repeat

By the end you can show which customer question exposed the gap, why it existed, what action was prioritized, who owned and shipped it, how the same question was re-tested, and what improved — or did not. Diagnosis matters: the answer may be missing content, unclear structure, technical accessibility, inconsistent information, or weak third-party coverage — not automatically “create more content.” An optional second loop can run in parallel as extra evidence, never a prerequisite.

The finish line

What you’ll have on Day 60

The pilot succeeds when you hold each of these — a defensible read on where you stand, and a clear call on what to do next.

  • An approved prompt set and defensible baseline you keep
  • One completed end-to-end GEO operating loop
  • A clear read on what improved, what did not, and the diagnosed reason
  • Measured AI-visibility impact, plus traffic, engagement, conversion, or revenue signals where integrations and observation windows allow
  • A documented, customer-owned process to measure, understand, act, validate, learn, and repeat
  • An evidence-based next-step recommendation: scale, iterate, hold, or stop

Working together

What we need — and how to start

The pilot is yours; Adobe runs alongside it. A small, senior working group and a fixed cadence keep sixty days on track.

From you

  • A named executive sponsor and a working owner.
  • Owners across the functions the opportunity may require — content, SEO, web, engineering, communications, analytics, and approvals.
  • Access to the domains, content, and data in scope.
  • Capacity to approve and ship one meaningful change during Activate & Measure.

From Adobe

  • The Adobe Brand Visibility toolset and the pilot method.
  • Market, competitive, prompt, presence, claims, and citation analysis for the agreed scope.
  • Prompt strategy, representative coverage, baseline and measurement setup, and the locked baseline.
  • The Day 60 evidence package and evidence-based next-step recommendation.

Getting started

To open Frame we need three things: a named sponsor, the one brand and market in scope, and a 45-minute kickoff. We agree the customer questions, proof criteria, and decision date up front — the sixty days do not drift.
