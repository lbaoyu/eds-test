The operating model

GEO is a standing operation, not a one-off

This is how a paid team runs GEO week in, week out: see where the brand stands in AI-powered discovery, ship a measured improvement, re-test against fixed goals, and feed the result into the next loop.

A visibility score is only the starting point. This playbook turns that score into a working process your team runs continuously — one loop at a time, several at once.

Operating principle

Measure → Understand → Act → Validate → Learn. Every opportunity stays tied to the question that revealed it — through to the change you ship and the result you measure.

Powered by Adobe Brand Visibility — Semrush-powered market and competitive intelligence feeds the GEO engine built by Adobe LLM Optimizer, connecting prompt strategy, brand presence and claims, optimization, collaboration, deployment, and impact measurement in one workflow. Exact capabilities depend on your entitlement and are confirmed with you during Frame.

By the end you can show which customer question exposed the gap, why it existed, what was prioritized, who shipped it, how it was re-tested, and what improved — or did not. Diagnosis matters: the answer may be missing content, unclear structure, technical accessibility, inconsistent information, or weak third-party coverage — not automatically “create more content.”

Running in parallel

One program, many loops at once

A paid program isn't a single loop — it's a portfolio of them. Scope each loop to one coherent slice, stagger the starts, and you get a steady drumbeat of readouts instead of one big-bang result at the end.

Scope one loop to one slice

  • One product or brand line — the card, the mortgage, the savings account — each with its own locked prompt set and pages.
  • Or one market / language (SG, TH, TW, HK): AI answers differ market to market.
  • Or one surface (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Overviews) where coverage diverges by engine.
  • One locked prompt set · one goal set · one owner · one measurement window. If a loop needs an “and,” it’s two loops.

Activation is the shared bottleneck

  • Discover and Define are cheap; Activate & Measure spends the scarce content, engineering, and Edge capacity.
  • Set a WIP limit on loops in Activate at once — many can sit in Discover/Define, few can ship at the same time.
  • The portfolio review decides which loop gets the next activation slot, not the calendar.

Cadence & backlog

Run two review rhythms: the deep per-loop readout at close, and a light portfolio review across loops — what’s in each phase, where activation capacity goes next, and what each closed loop feeds. Every readout’s next steps (a follow-on page tier, a v2 prompt cohort, an Iterate carried forward) drop into a shared loop backlog that the portfolio review prioritizes into the next start. That’s the outer loop: loops feed the backlog, the backlog starts the next loops.

What each loop yields

What every loop leaves behind

A loop closes when you hold each of these — a defensible read, a measured result, and a clear call on what carries into the next loop.

  • An approved prompt set and defensible baseline you keep
  • One closed GEO loop, end to end
  • A clear read on what improved, what did not, and the diagnosed reason
  • Measured AI-visibility impact, plus traffic, engagement, conversion, or revenue signals where integrations and observation windows allow
  • A documented, repeatable loop your team runs again — each pass faster
  • An evidence-based call — scale, iterate, hold, or stop — that seeds the next loop

Running the program

What it takes to run GEO continuously

The program is yours; Adobe runs alongside it. A small, senior working group and a steady cadence keep loops moving and readouts landing.

From you

  • A named executive sponsor, a GEO program owner across loops, and an owner for each live loop.
  • Owners across the functions the opportunity may require — content, SEO, web, engineering, communications, analytics, and approvals.
  • Access to the domains, content, and data in scope.
  • Capacity to approve and ship changes during each loop’s Activate & Measure — the shared bottleneck to protect.

From Adobe

  • The Adobe Brand Visibility toolset and the operating method.
  • Market, competitive, prompt, presence, claims, and citation analysis for the agreed scope.
  • Prompt strategy, representative coverage, and the locked baseline and measurement setup.
  • Per-loop readouts and the portfolio view across loops.

Starting your first loop

To open Frame on your first loop we need three things: a named sponsor, the one brand and market in scope, and a 45-minute kickoff. Agree the customer questions, proof criteria, and loop-close decision up front — then stagger the next loop a few weeks behind.
