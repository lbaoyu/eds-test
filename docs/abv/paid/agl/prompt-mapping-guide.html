Branded vs. non-branded

Let ABV detect branded prompts

In short

Tracking branded vs. non-branded is the right goal — but ABV does it for you, so don’t spend a category on it.

Why · 1

It’s detected for you

ABV labels each prompt branded or non-branded. The Prompt Library shows the balance and flags when the branded share drifts outside a healthy range.

Why · 2

You can already filter by it

Brand Presence filters by branded vs. non-branded, so a category for it would duplicate a built-in view.

Why · 3

Categories anchor history

Changing categories later resets historical data. Keep them for a stable dimension: AGL’s product lines.

“Do not add categories/filters for brand/non-brand/geographic as you can filter specifically for this in the Brand Presence dashboard.” Adobe Brand Visibility best practices, Experience League
+

Add AGL plan and product names as brand aliases. In Brands Management › Aliases, add names such as Three for Free so mentions of them count toward AGL.

Intent & custom tags

Use tags for AGL’s own views

In short

ABV classifies intent natively. Add a Funnel tag only to split Awareness from Informational — and use more tags for state and plan.

Built in · Intent

What ABV classifies on its own

Every prompt gets one of five intents, and the Prompt Library tracks intent coverage.

  • Informational
  • Navigational
  • Commercial
  • Transactional
  • Task
Your choice · Funnel tag

Why add a funnel tag

ABV would treat both “How can I lower my electricity bill…” and “What is the daily supply charge…” as Informational.

If AGL wants to report Awareness separately, as your list does, tag it.

Suggested tag groups

A prompt can carry several tags, so these stack.

TagSub-tagsExample prompt
FunnelAwareness · Informational · CommercialHow can I lower my electricity bill without being cold in winter?
StateNSW · QLD · VIC · SA · ACTWho has the cheapest electricity rates in NSW?
PlanThree for Free · other AGL plansHow does the AGL Three for Free plan work?
FeatureGreenPower · other featuresDoes AGL offer GreenPower so I can match my electricity use with renewable energy?
tags column Funnel > Commercial; State > NSW
  • Separate tags with a semicolon ;
  • Nest a sub-tag with >, as in Tag > Sub-tag
  • Tags that don’t exist yet are created on import
  • Avoid reserved names: category tag intent origin type source — hence Funnel, not Intent

Next steps

From list to live tracking

  1. Consolidate the full list

    Use the four columns above across your priority product lines. Save as a UTF-8 CSV.

  2. Send it over for a quick check

    Even a rough first cut. We’ll sanity-check subcategories, tags, aliases, and formatting before loading.

  3. Import the CSV

    Prompt Management›Prompt Library›select the AGL brand and market›Import CSV

  4. Allow up to 24 hours, then review

    New prompts need processing before they appear in Brand Presence. Then check the Branded / Unbranded and Intent coverage tiles in the Prompt Library.
