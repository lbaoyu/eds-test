Generative engine optimization (GEO) helps you improve how your brand is found, described, and cited in AI answers.

ABV is designed to be the best enterprise GEO tool, uniquely connecting the loop end to end: set a business goal and baseline, prioritize and deploy improvements, verify what went live, measure impact, and choose the next action.

GEO builds on SEO. Keep your search foundations; add AI visibility, answer quality, and business-outcome measurement to the workflow.
© 2026 Adobe. All Rights Reserved.

The differentiator is the closed loop, not any single dashboard. Many tools stop at monitoring; ABV goes through deployment, verification, and measurement to a decision.

Section 3 breaks this ring into six working steps — Frame, Curate, Baseline, Plan, Implement, Review — used for each GEO loop.

Reassure SEO teams: GEO extends SEO, it does not replace it.

02How ABV works

Each layer has a distinct job

Semrush · Market intelligence

Choose the right focus

Semrush Enterprise AIO brings market and competitor context. Narrow broad prompt demand to a relevant, stable cohort aligned with one business goal.

Adobe · Optimization

Deploy changes and validate

Track Brand Presence and Claims, prioritize work in Opportunity Workspace, and deploy through Optimize at Edge, Optimize at Source, or your publishing process. Validate what went live.

Together · One ABV workflow

Run the complete GEO loop

Combine Semrush intelligence and Adobe optimization: set a business goal, curate prompts, baseline, plan, deploy and validate, then evaluate results and business impact to decide next.

© 2026 Adobe. All Rights Reserved.

Simple mental model: Semrush picks where to play, Adobe changes and validates, ABV ties both into one loop with measurement.

The split is about each layer’s job, not where the data comes from — Brand Presence is tracked in the Adobe workflow but runs on Semrush-powered data (Part 2).

02How ABV works · architecture

Each workflow uses the inputs it needs

Inputs · scope and evidence

Set the scope

Brands, markets, domains, and tracked prompts define monitoring. CDN logs add agent requests and human referrals; supported analytics connections add business outcomes. Connect only what the workflow requires.

Analysis · distinct signals

Find what to investigate

Prompt responses inform Brand Presence; site and traffic analysis inform URL investigation and opportunities. Keep mentions, citations, agent requests, and human referrals distinct — not one combined score.

Action · three delivery routes

Route approved work

Opportunities proposes changes; Workspace coordinates owners and progress. Deliver supported agent-facing changes via Optimize at Edge, push authoring updates via Optimize at Source, or route to engineering and offsite owners.

Insight is not delivery or impact. A recommendation or Workspace status proves neither.
© 2026 Adobe. All Rights Reserved.

Key discipline: four signals stay separate — mentions, citations, agent requests, human referrals. And a recommendation ≠ shipped ≠ impact.

04Prepare · Choose what to track

Two views. Different decisions.

Market view

AI Visibility

Where should we focus?

Prompts used: prompts based on Semrush’s market intelligence (0–100 monthly snapshot) — not your tracked cohort.

Use it to: explore demand, compare competitors, and choose opportunities at kickoff or between loops.

VS

Tracked view

Brand Presence

How is our focus area performing?

Prompts used: the prompts you select and track in your brand’s Prompt Library.

Use it to: capture a baseline, monitor brand presence, and compare results for a stable cohort within each loop.

Choose the focus with AI Visibility. Follow progress with Brand Presence. Different prompt sets mean different scores — compare trends within each view, not scores between them.
© 2026 Adobe. All Rights Reserved.

Important distinction: two different prompt populations produce two different scores. Never treat AI Visibility as the “before” and Brand Presence as the “after”.

04Prepare · Choose what to track

Source and curate focused prompts

© 2026 Adobe. All Rights Reserved.

Four candidate sources feed exploration: Semrush AI Visibility, Google Search Console, Synthetic Personas, Citation Attempt. Prompt Strategy refines; only selected prompts enter the Prompt Library and get tracked in Brand Presence.

04Prepare · Choose what to track

Explore, plan, then track

Explore · AI Visibility

Find relevant opportunities

Use Semrush to identify visibility gaps and relevant topics. Add search-query evidence, persona-led questions, and citation insights as inputs to review — not prompts to track automatically.

Plan · Prompt Strategy

Turn opportunities into focus

Review candidates against the business goal, audience, intent, product or service, and market. Refine wording, remove duplicates and out-of-scope prompts, and select a balanced cohort.

Track · Brand Presence

Measure a defined prompt set

Only selected prompts move into the Prompt Library. Establish a baseline and keep the cohort stable within each loop so results remain comparable as changes are made.

Use the views together: AI Visibility informs where to look; Prompt Strategy helps define what to track; Brand Presence follows the selected focus over time.
© 2026 Adobe. All Rights Reserved.

Part 2 demos each of these views live. Here, land the principle: curate deliberately, then hold the cohort stable.

05Act · Levers and delivery

Different levers for different visibility gaps

Prioritize work that addresses an evidenced visibility gap and supports your business goal. Areas can overlap — assign each action a clear owner and delivery path.
© 2026 Adobe. All Rights Reserved.

Owned site · content: answer priority questions with evidence; keep details consistent. Owned site · technical: AI-bot access, readable content, fix 404/3xx/5xx. Off-site: credible third-party references. Commerce: consistent names, attributes, availability, pricing, policies.

05Act · Levers and delivery

Deliver approved optimizations to AI agents

Step 1 · routing

Configure the boundary

IT/CDN sets routing at the outermost CDN for selected paths and allows the AdobeEdgeOptimize/1.0 fetcher through robots, WAF, and bot-management controls.

Step 2 · approval

Review and deploy

Confirm enabled types, such as content recovery, summaries, and FAQs. Preview and refine where supported, then deploy approved URLs. Eligibility depends on the account.

Step 3 · control

Verify live delivery

Check what routed agents receive, record scope and date, and roll back when needed. Origin CMS and human/search delivery stay unchanged.

© 2026 Adobe. All Rights Reserved.

Humans and search crawlers (including Googlebot / Google AI Overviews) get the normal origin page; routed AI agents (ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude) get the edge-optimized HTML. For origin authoring updates (H1 & Meta Tags, Content Freshness), use Optimize at Source on AEMaaCS or EDS and publish through your normal review.

Caveat: deployment does not guarantee uplift or an IME report — IME requires 20+ High Potential URLs on RCV, Table of Contents, or LLM-friendly Summaries.

06Prove · Measure value

Match the funnel to your business goal

Discovery · foundation + 01–02

Enter the category shortlist

For a product launch or priority market, make key pages accessible and improve presence on the prompts that matter.

Evidence: readable content, successful agent access, brand mentions, and visibility.

Trust · 03–04

Be accurate and credible

For complex products or policy-sensitive content, improve factual representation and make authoritative pages easier to cite.

Evidence: reviewed answer accuracy, sentiment, citations, and cited URLs.

Growth · 05–06

Earn qualified engagement

For acquisition or commerce goals, connect visits from AI answers with useful engagement, leads, conversions, or revenue.

Evidence: baseline vs. post-deployment analytics and AI referrals; contribution, not causal proof.

One goal per loop. Agree the business measure and the upstream signals needed to interpret it.
Fix the foundation first. Gaps in access limit every layer above — check them early.
Connect the evidence. Agentic Traffic needs CDN log forwarding; Business Impact needs AA, CJA, or GA4 and entitlement.
© 2026 Adobe. All Rights Reserved.

Pick the funnel band from the business goal, not the other way round. Discovery for launches, Trust for regulated/complex products, Growth for acquisition/commerce.

07Get ready · ways of working

Agree how decisions get made

Operating approach

Centralized

A central ABV team coordinates priorities, standards, and decisions; delivery teams carry out approved work.

Federated

A central team sets shared direction and governance while business units or markets plan and execute locally.

Decentralized

Individual teams lead their own ABV work within shared enterprise principles and agreed guardrails.

Decision path

01

Recommend

GEO/SEO and content propose prioritized opportunities and changes.

02

Approve

Business owner + reviewers sign off scope, change, and timing.

03

Execute

Content, web, or IT/CDN deliver through the agreed route.

04

Review

Analytics + business owner confirm what shipped and assess results.

Working rhythm: a short weekly working session; route decisions through existing forums; keep one shared record of decisions, actions, owners, and outcomes.
