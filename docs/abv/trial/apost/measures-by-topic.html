Reporting model

Three questions, three sources

ABV’s Brand Presence view is the main place to report results for the tracked prompts. Read the Visibility Score as a headline number and use the metrics behind it to explain changes. It doesn’t show whether answers are correct: accuracy, completeness and freshness need a manual review against Australia Post-approved information.

ABV Brand Presence

Is Australia Post visible?

Visibility Score
The headline number. Explain changes with the metrics below.
Mentions
Whether Australia Post appears in the answer.
Citations
Whether the answer cites Australia Post pages. Owned citation share is the share of citations that point to auspost.com.au.
Sentiment
Whether Australia Post is described positively, neutrally or negatively.
Share of Voice
Australia Post’s visibility against competitors. Read it on non-branded prompts, the questions that don’t name Australia Post. Market Tracking compares up to five competitors.
Trends
Change over time.
ABV Brand Claims

What is AI saying?

Claims
The individual statements AI systems make about Australia Post, grouped when repeated.
Claim sentiment
Whether each claim is favorable, neutral or unfavorable.
Sources
The pages and sites each claim relies on.
How Brand Claims helps
Manual review

Is it right?

Accuracy
The answer matches Australia Post-approved information.
Completeness
It includes the steps, options and conditions the customer needs.
Freshness
Dates, hours and conditions are current.
Citation support
Each citation backs up the claim it’s attached to.

Scored by hand on a sample of answers. These aren’t ABV dashboard scores.

Trial objectives

How each trial objective is measured

The three objectives in Australia Post’s brief, and the metrics that answer each one.

  • Objective 01

    Accuracy of AI answers

    Are answers about products, services and support accurate, complete and up to date?

    Manual reviewABV Brand Claims
    • Score a sample of answers for accuracy, completeness and freshness, and check that each citation supports its claim.
    • Track owned citation share as an early sign that answers rely on Australia Post pages.
    • Use Brand Claims to pick which answers to review first.

    Result: wrong or missing information, and the content to fix or strengthen.

  • Objective 02

    Sentiment across topics and intents

    Is Australia Post described positively, neutrally or negatively, and does that change by topic, customer need or AI platform?

    ABV Brand PresenceABV Brand Claims
    • Compare sentiment by topic, AI platform, and branded versus non-branded prompts.
    • Use Brand Claims to see which statements make sentiment positive or negative.
    • The trial can’t filter by intent, so add the intent to topic names at setup, for example “Tracking · Support”.

    Result: where sentiment differs by topic, customer need or AI platform.

  • Objective 03

    Competitive benchmarking

    How do Australia Post’s offerings compare with competitors, and where are others recommended first?

    ABV Brand Presence
    • Read Visibility Score, mentions and Share of Voice on non-branded prompts. Branded questions make Australia Post look stronger than it is.
    • Compare up to five competitors in Market Tracking. Use AI Visibility for wider market context only.
    • In the manual review, record where Australia Post ranks when options are listed.

    Result: where competitors are recommended ahead of Australia Post, and what content could change that.

Brand Claims

Use Brand Claims to focus the manual review

Brand Claims pulls out the individual statements AI systems make about Australia Post in the tracked-prompt answers. It shows what is being said, not whether it is true.

ABV Brand ClaimsGenerally available

What is being said?

  • Repeated claims grouped together, so the review can start with the common ones.
  • Each claim’s sentiment, category and cited sources.
  • Whether claims rely on Australia Post pages or other sites, such as YouTube, Reddit and Wikipedia.
  • Suggested content actions, which can be saved to ABV’s Opportunity Workspace.
Fact CheckingEarly access

Is it backed by evidence?

  • Checks selected claims against Australia Post’s site and the open web, not an approved fact set.
  • Labels each claim Supported, Contradicted, Not verified or Not checked.
  • Only checks claims that also get recommendations, so many show Not checked.
Limits of Fact Checking
  • A verdict reflects only the evidence found.
  • Not verified doesn’t mean false.
  • Wrong claims with no evidence online aren’t flagged.

Confirm early access is turned on for Australia Post before relying on it.

Claims only come from the tracked prompts. A claim about something the prompts don’t cover won’t appear.

Using the measures

One job per measure

  • ABV Brand PresenceReports results

    Visibility Score, mentions, citations, sentiment and Share of Voice for the tracked prompts, by topic and AI platform.

  • Brand ClaimsFocuses the review

    Shows which claims to check first and which content to fix.

  • Fact CheckingShortlists likely errors · early access

    Where turned on, Contradicted claims give the manual review a starting list. Verdicts aren’t accuracy scores.

  • Prompt Strategy and Prompt LibraryChecks setup

    Confirm the 100 prompts cover the selected topics and customer needs. Coverage is a setup check, not a result.

  • AI VisibilityAdds market context

    Wider market and topic data to help choose prompts. Don’t report it in place of tracked-prompt results.

  • Manual reviewScores accuracy

    A sample of answers scored for accuracy, completeness and freshness, with each citation checked against its claim.
