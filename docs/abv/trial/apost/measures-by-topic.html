Reporting model

One reporting surface, one manual check

ABV’s Brand Presence view is the main reporting surface for tracked-prompt Visibility Score, mentions, citations, sentiment, competitor comparisons and trends. Treat the Visibility Score as a headline indicator and use its component metrics to understand movement; it is not a factual-accuracy score. Accuracy, completeness and freshness require a manual answer review against Australia Post-approved information.

Whether the answer is right

Against Australia Post-approved information, on a sample of responses.

  • AccuracyClaims match Australia Post-approved information.
  • CompletenessThe answer includes the steps, options and conditions the customer needs.
  • FreshnessDates, deadlines, hours and conditions are current for the place and period.
  • Citation supportEach citation supports the claim it accompanies.

These are not ABV dashboard scores.

Read it this way

The Visibility Score shows whether Australia Post shows up in answers to tracked prompts. It is not a factual-accuracy score. Only the manual review shows whether what the answer says is right.

Trial objectives

Where each trial objective is measured

The three objectives from Australia Post’s brief, mapped to the measure that answers each one.

Objective 01

Accuracy of AI responses

Are answers about products, services, and help and support accurate, complete and up to date?

Measured mainly inManual review
  • Score a sample of responses for accuracy, completeness and freshness against Australia Post-approved information.
  • Check that each citation supports the claim it accompanies.
  • This is the first priority for parcel tracking, missed deliveries and collection, Post Office and peak-period topics, and the app.
OutputIncorrect and missing information, and the content to clarify or strengthen.
Objective 02

Sentiment across topics and intents

Is Australia Post described positively, negatively or neutrally, and does that vary by topic, customer need or platform?

Measured inABV Brand Presence
  • Report sentiment for the tracked prompts; compare by topic and available AI platform.
  • Sentiment is a supporting measure for fast and same-day delivery and the app, and is also reported for small-business shipping.
  • Reading sentiment by intent depends on prompt coverage: confirm each topic’s prompts cover the intended customer needs at setup.
OutputWhere portrayal varies by topic, customer need or AI platform.
Objective 03

Competitive benchmarking

How are Australia Post’s offerings represented against competitors, and where are others recommended first?

Measured inABV Brand Presence
  • Visibility Score, mentions, citations and competitor comparisons on the same tracked prompts.
  • This is the first priority for fast and same-day delivery and for small-business shipping / MyPost Business.
  • During manual review, record Australia Post’s position when options are listed.
  • Use AI Visibility for broader market context, not as a substitute for tracked-prompt results.
OutputWhere competitors lead, and where content and market presence can be strengthened.

Using the measures

How to use the measures in the trial

Each measure has one job. Keep outcome reporting, setup checks, market context and manual scoring separate.

ABV Brand Presence

Outcome reporting

Report Visibility Score, mentions, citations, sentiment and competitor comparisons for the same tracked prompts. Compare trends by topic and available AI platform.

Prompt Strategy / Prompt Library

Setup check

Confirm the 100 prompts cover all six topics and the intended customer needs. Prompt coverage is a setup check, not a GEO outcome.

AI Visibility

Market context

Use broader market and topic insights to inform prompt selection. Don’t substitute this broader view for results on Australia Post’s tracked prompts.

Manual answer review

Manual scoring

Score a sample of responses for accuracy, completeness and freshness. Also check whether each citation supports the claim it accompanies. These are not ABV dashboard scores.
