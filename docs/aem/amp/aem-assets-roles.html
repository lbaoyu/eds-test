Access principles

Assign access according to the work

This reference covers AEM Assets Ultimate, including its user types and Content Hub and Production Author profile assignments. The role guidance below is specific to Assets Ultimate and should not be assumed to apply to other AEM Assets editions.

AEM Assets Ultimate access should be assigned according to what each person needs to do. Content Hub access is for finding and using approved assets; AEM Assets authoring access is for working directly with the DAM. A product profile grants a user's entitlement and baseline role, while AEM group and folder permissions can further control what content they can access and what actions they can perform.

Content HubFind and use approved assets

For approved-asset discovery and consumption, with explicitly enabled contribution workflows where applicable.

AEM Assets authoringWork directly with the DAM

For asset operations, metadata, and governance, with permissions scoped to the team's responsibilities.

Role reference

AEM Assets Ultimate user types and role boundaries

User typeSuitable personasCapabilities and boundaries
Limited UserSales teams, executives, franchisees, regional partners, and other asset consumersAccesses, finds, views, and uses approved assets through Content Hub. Does not access the AEM Assets author environment or create, edit, or manage DAM assets.
CollaboratorMarketing, social, legal, and creative contributorsUses assets through supported integrations and Content Hub, and can create or edit assets using supported Adobe Express and Firefly workflows. This role does not provide full DAM management, metadata governance, or permission administration. Content Hub contribution or upload capabilities must be enabled and permissioned for the intended workflow.
Power UserDAM librarians, brand managers, and content operationsWorks directly with AEM Assets to manage assets, metadata, and asset governance or automation. This role is for DAM operations; it does not replace the Administrator role for managing user access and content permissions.
AdministratorDesignated DAM owners, IT, or operations staffManages users, roles, content permissions, and governance. Keep this role limited to people with administrative responsibilities.

Adobe's Assets Ultimate guidance distinguishes the Collaborator's Content Hub, integration, and Express/Firefly capabilities from the Power User's broader DAM management capabilities. It reserves managing permissions on AEM Assets content for Administrators.

AMP recommendations

Which Assets Ultimate roles fit AMP's use cases?

Approved-asset consumption

Marketing teams that need to find, download, and use approved assets

  • Use Limited User for people whose need is to discover and use approved assets through Content Hub.
  • Use Collaborator for people who also use supported integrations or Express/Firefly workflows.
  • Use Power User for the subset who directly organize DAM content, manage metadata, or oversee asset operations.
Asset contribution & operations

Marketing teams that need to upload and manage assets

“Manage” is the key distinction. For people responsible for adding assets directly to AEM Assets, organizing them, or maintaining their metadata, Power User is the best fit. If people are contributing through a specific Content Hub workflow, enable and permission that workflow explicitly; Collaborator does not imply unrestricted upload or management of the DAM.

Migration & ongoing management

Creative teams migrating SharePoint assets and managing them over time

Use Power User for the people performing the direct AEM DAM work—ingesting and organizing assets, maintaining metadata, and supporting ongoing asset operations. Use Collaborator for other creatives who work through supported integrations, Content Hub, or Express/Firefly workflows without managing the DAM.

Assigning a profile grants access; it does not itself migrate SharePoint content or provide a migration tool.

Adobe recommends managing access through user groups and folder permissions, so access can be scoped to the content each team needs.

Admin Console assignments

Content Hub and Production Author profile assignments

For AEM Assets Ultimate, a Limited User needs the AEM Assets Limited Users profile for Content Hub. For a Collaborator, Power User, or Administrator, assign that Content Hub profile and the matching role profile on the Production Author instance:

RoleContent Hub profileProduction Author profile
Limited UserAEM Assets Limited UsersNot required
CollaboratorAEM Assets Limited UsersAEM Assets Collaborator Users
Power UserAEM Assets Limited UsersAEM Assets Power Users
AdministratorAEM Assets Limited UsersAEM Administrators

A Limited User does not need the corresponding Production Author profile.

Sources: Assets Ultimate — What can a Limited User do in AEM Assets Ultimate? · Assets Ultimate — What are available user types and privileges?

An AEM Users profile, if present in Admin Console, is a separate general AEM profile; it is not the same as the Assets Ultimate Limited User, Collaborator, or Power User assignments.

Source: AEM as a Cloud Service Team and Product Profiles — Environment and Tier Level Product Instances

Separate access boundaries

AEM Assets Ultimate access does not automatically grant AEM Sites authoring

AEM Assets Ultimate access should not be treated as AEM Sites page-authoring access. People who author site pages need the appropriate AEM Sites author profile and permissions for the relevant environment. A person who needs both DAM and Sites access may need profiles for both. Site publishing and content permissions should be managed as part of the Sites workflow, separately from an Assets Ultimate role.

Source: AEM as a Cloud Service Team and Product Profiles — Environment and Tier Level Product Instances

In summary

For AEM Assets Ultimate, use Limited User for approved-asset consumption in Content Hub, Collaborator for supported contribution and creation workflows, Power User for direct DAM work, and Administrator for access and permission governance. The right mix can be adjusted as AMP's responsibilities and needs evolve.
