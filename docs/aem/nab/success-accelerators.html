Success Accelerator 1

SA 1Native app personalization: use cases and pilot design

Use Case Mapping to Solution CapabilityAdobe Target · AEM as a Cloud Service~40 hours

What you shared

  • You'd like to test different copy and links on native app screens such as Explore.
  • Target isn't used within native app screens yet.
  • The native app guide shared alongside this proposal sets out a possible direction and is the starting point here.
Objective

Turn the direction into an agreed, prioritized pilot design that your app teams can plan around.

Activities
  • A workshop with your personalization and app teams to validate the direction
  • Prioritizing screens, slots and use cases, each with a KPI
  • A high-level review of the app's current SDK, identity and consent setup
  • Options and trade-offs for the SDK extension, content delivery and QA approach
Deliverables
  • A use case and slot backlog with KPIs
  • A pilot design brief: slot, content model, audiences, success metric and QA approach
  • A readout
What we'd need from you

Your app product owner, a mobile developer lead, the Target owner, and an overview of the current SDK and release process.

Out of scope

App development and SDK implementation.

Success Accelerator 2

SA 2Personalization authoring patterns and template

Solution ReviewAEM as a Cloud Service (Sites)~40 hours

What you shared

  • A personalization-specific template was the improvement you felt would have the greatest impact.
  • Small content differences currently need a new Experience Fragment each time.
  • Existing patterns can feel restrictive, and reuse gets harder as the library grows.
  • Elevate components aren't available in AEM yet.
Objective

Recommend a reusable authoring pattern that separates experience structure from variable content and reduces manual setup.

Activities
  • Reviewing a sample of current personalization XFs, templates and components
  • Walking through how an author builds an XF for an activity today
  • Assessing the options: XF templates and policies, XF variations vs. Content Fragment variations, and CFs inside XFs
  • Considering how Elevate components could fit in once they're available
Deliverables
  • Current-state findings
  • A guide on when to use an XF vs. a Content Fragment
  • A template specification for your developers: structure, responsive behavior, styles, badge and author guardrails
  • An approach for baselining the success measures you named: consistency, asset creation speed and activity delivery time
  • A readout
What we'd need from you

Authors, your AEM developer lead, sample XFs, and a design system contact.

Out of scope

Building the template or components.

Success Accelerator 3

SA 3Personalization in secure channels and dynamic experiences

Adoption ReviewAdobe Target~40 hours

What you shared

  • The two NAB Connect mbox locations rely on hard-coded experiences that can break when the HTML changes, and product promotion content is hard to keep current.
  • Internet Banking is being Target-enabled next.
  • Content inserted into forms can be replaced when customers move back and forth between steps.
  • Mini-apps don't always work when exported as Experience Fragments.
  • Experiences that span much of a page can render incorrectly when the default page changes.
Objective

Understand the root causes and outline options for more resilient personalization in these scenarios.

Activities
  • Working sessions covering the Target implementation in NAB Connect, Internet Banking and forms: library, version and delivery approach
  • Reviewing the two NAB Connect locations, one form and one broad-page activity
  • Root-cause analysis, then options with trade-offs for each scenario
Deliverables
  • A findings and root-cause summary
  • Options and considerations for each scenario
  • A checklist for enabling new Target locations, such as Internet Banking
  • A readout
What we'd need from you

NAB Connect and Internet Banking developer leads, the Target admin, and examples shared through screen-shares or a QA environment.

Out of scope

Code changes.

Success Accelerator 4

SA 4Personalization content governance and lifecycle

Tool Workflow & Governance OptimizationAEM as a Cloud Service · Adobe Target~40 hours

What you shared

  • AEM workflows aren't currently used for approvals, localization or expiry of personalized content.
  • As the library grows, it's hard for authors to know what already exists.
  • Some content is maintained in both AEM and Target.
  • Unpublishing an Experience Fragment in AEM doesn't retire the matching offer in Target.
Objective

A lightweight governance model for personalization content, from request to retirement.

Activities
  • Mapping the current process: roles, legal and brand approvals, and localization
  • Reviewing folder structure, naming, tagging, and how Target workspaces and offers are organized
  • Recommending a lightweight workflow that includes scheduling, expiry and a retirement step in Target
Deliverables
  • Current vs. proposed process maps
  • A RACI showing who is responsible, accountable, consulted and informed at each step
  • Naming, folder and tagging conventions
  • An offer lifecycle checklist
  • A readout
What we'd need from you

Your personalization lead, authors, the AEM and Target admins, and optionally a legal or brand representative.

Out of scope

Implementing workflow or content changes.
