Start with the question

Two visibility views answer different questions

ABV combines broad market intelligence with your organization’s own tracked prompt set. Think of the first as a way to choose where to focus; the second is how you follow the topics you have chosen.

How to read this paid-customer tour. It highlights the enhanced experience and workflows relevant to paid ABV customers; it does not imply every capability is paid-exclusive. Some foundational views have trial counterparts. Feature access, available data, and deployment depend on your organization’s entitlement, rollout, and connected-site setup.

Market discovery · Semrush data

AI Visibility

Explore how brands appear across AI answers for a broad market prompt set. Start immediately—without first configuring your own prompts.

Best question: What topics, brands, and sources should we pay attention to?

Ongoing monitoring · your tracked prompts

Brand Presence

Measure your brand against the prompts in your Prompt Library, then review changes across the platforms and markets configured for your account.

Best question: How are we showing up for the conversations we chose to track?

Keep the lenses distinct. AI Visibility and Brand Presence use different prompt scopes and metric methods. Use each view for its intended decision; don’t treat their scores as a direct before-and-after comparison.

Find prompts worth tracking

Prompt Strategy turns discovery into tracking candidates

Prompt Strategy is the review queue for potential additions. It surfaces suggested topics and prompts from available sources so your team can decide what deserves ongoing measurement—before anything enters the tracked set.

Starts with
Market research plus available connected or generated prompt sources.
Explore
Recommended topics and prompts, where each came from, and the gap or presence it signals.
Use the result for
Choosing deliberate additions to the tracked prompt set.

Source

Know where a suggestion comes from

Each recommendation is labeled with one of four sources, as available in your account. The source tells you what kind of evidence sits behind it—see the breakdown below.

Fit

Check it against your plan

Review the individual prompts behind a suggestion. Keep the ones that match your business, audience, market, language, and measurement goals.

Track

Promote the keepers

Choose Track to add selected prompts to the active library. Recommendations can reveal new conversations, competitor gaps, or areas where your brand already appears.

Suggestion sources

  • SemrushSearch and competitor data, grouped into strategic categories—such as topics you already win versus opportunities to grow.
  • Google Search ConsolePrompts derived from your own Search Console data, grounded in how people already find your site. Refresh on demand.
  • Synthetic PersonasPrompt sets written from the perspective of different audience personas, covering journeys keyword data can miss.
  • Citation AttemptPrompts aimed at the pages where you want to earn citations, so priority URLs get measured.

Key actions in this view

  • SourceFilter the queue: Semrush, Google Search Console, Synthetic Personas, Citation Attempt
  • TrackAdd selected prompts, or the whole recommendation, after a confirmation
  • DismissRemove a suggestion, optionally with a reason
  • RefreshPull fresh Search Console suggestions on demand, subject to a cooldown
Recommendations are candidates, not a plan. Available sources depend on your account’s connections. Size additions against the prompt allowance in your agreement, and treat each Track decision as a change to what Brand Presence measures.

Monitor what matters to your brand

Brand Presence shows how the tracked cohort changes

For paid customers, the enhanced Brand Presence experience powered by Semrush runs the active prompt set on a recurring basis. Use it to review trends and understand how your brand appears in responses—not as a guaranteed measure of business outcomes.

Starts with
Your active Prompt Library, market, language, and platform scope.
Explore
Brand Visibility, Share of Voice, Source Visibility, topics, and response evidence.
Use the result for
Monitoring the chosen conversation set and spotting a change to investigate.
Brand VisibilityHow frequently your brand is mentioned across monitored prompts.
Source VisibilityHow often your website is cited in AI-generated responses.
Share of VoiceVisibility weighted by search demand and ranking position.
Topic VolumeMarket search demand associated with a topic—not a count of private AI prompts.
Aspect sentimentDifferent aspects in one response can carry different sentiment; one label may not describe the whole answer.
Mentions / CitationsCurrent response-based measures count responses containing a mention or citation, not every repeated occurrence.

Key actions in this view

  • FilterDate range, market-language, platform, and category
  • Compare brandsAdd competitors to the mention and citation trends
  • ExpandDetailsTopic → prompts → the individual AI responses
  • Configure ColumnsChoose the metrics shown in tables
  • ExportDownload tables as CSV for analysis
Explain the metric transition. Brand Visibility replaces the former Visibility Score in the enhanced experience. If a customer is moving from a legacy view, compare like-for-like periods and explain definition changes first; historical scores and occurrence counts may not map one-to-one to current measures.

Read the pattern

Move from roll-up to evidence

Start with the overall direction, then segment by the market-language, platform, category, prompt, and time views available in your account. Inspect prompt-level responses, mentions, citations, topic context, and competitor patterns before deciding what to change.

Keep comparisons useful

Use a consistent scope

For a fair trend, keep the tracked prompts, market-language, platform selection, and time windows comparable. Expanded prompt coverage can improve representation of your business, but it also changes what the dashboard measures.

Brand Claims · understand AI’s representation

Brand Claims organizes what tracked AI responses say about you

For paid customers, Brand Claims moves the question from “our brand appeared” to “what did the response assert?” It extracts claims from responses to prompts in your Brand Presence set and groups them so teams can review recurring perceptions rather than scan an unstructured response wall.

Starts with
Responses to prompts tracked in Brand Presence—not all conversations across the web.
Explore
Claim text, sentiment, category, brand relevance, claim type, recurrence, responses, and cited sources.
Use the result for
Validating a perception and deciding whether it warrants follow-up.

Organize the response narrative

Find the claims that recur

Review the assertion, sentiment, business category, brand relevance, claim type, and recurrence. One response may produce multiple claims, so a claim count is not a prompt count.

Investigate the evidence

Open responses and sources

Trace a claim to the AI response where it appeared and inspect the sources cited in that response. Use the evidence to frame the question—not as independent proof that the claim is true or false.

Key actions in this view

  • FilterWith opportunitiesNarrow All Claims; surface claims linked to offsite opportunities
  • DetailsOpen a claim from Top claims or All Claims
  • OverviewRecommendationsResponsesRead context, suggested actions, and the source responses
  • Owned actionsEarned actionsSeparate site fixes from external outreach
  • Create this StrategyTurn a recommendation into a strategy in Opportunity Workspace
  • FeedbackRate claim quality with thumbs up/down and a comment
Availability and interpretation. Brand Claims is generally available to paid customers; release guidance has also described limited trial run access. Confirm the customer’s current tenant and entitlement rather than inferring access from a visible surface. Brand Claims surfaces assertions; it does not fact-check them. A strategy’s deployment status does not prove impact or claim resolution.

Starts with
CDN log forwarding configured for an onboarded domain.
Explore
Request trends, agent types and platforms, success rates, movers, and per-URL performance.
Use the result for
Spotting access failures and the pages agents care about most.

Trend

Weekly request volume by platform and agent type. Top Movers and Bottom Movers show which URLs are gaining or losing agent attention.

Agents

See which crawlers and chatbots request your pages. Separate crawlers from chatbots and compare platforms, which can include ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Meta AI, and GitHub Copilot.

Health

Check request volume and success rate per URL. Open Details for that page’s citations, topics, and agentic and referral trend.

Key actions in this view

A request is not a visit—or influence. Agent requests are automated retrievals, not people. Some answers use cached or indexed content without a live fetch to your site. Access problems surface as opportunities such as Agentic Traffic Errors and Traffic Blocked by robots.txt.

Opportunities turn a diagnosed gap into a proposed action

An opportunity is a recommendation tied to a page, source, or visibility gap. Review the evidence and proposed change, then decide whether the work fits your priorities, approval path, and delivery route.

Technical GEO

Help agents reach and read your pages

Recover Content Visibility finds content hidden from agents by client-side rendering and can be fixed at the edge. Traffic Blocked by robots.txt and Agentic Traffic Errors (404, 403, 5xx) point to fixes made in your own stack.

Content and structure

Make pages easier to use in answers

Add LLM-friendly Summaries, Add Relevant FAQs, Simplify Complex Content, and Add Table of Contents, plus Add Multimedia Transcript Summaries (Early Access). Suggestions can be edited before delivery.

Offsite

Strengthen the sources AI cites

Cited Web Sources, YouTube, and Reddit Visibility & Reputation Analysis, plus Wikipedia Analysis. They appear only when relevant citations or articles exist, and inform earned work—ABV does not edit third-party properties.

Adobe Commerce

Strengthen product information

Enrich Product Detail Pages recovers catalog details missing from agent-visible PDPs at the edge. Enrich Product Catalog (Beta) writes improved names and descriptions to the catalog itself, so every channel sees them.

  • ShareDismissAgentic Traffic Errors: route or close a suggestion
Eligibility varies by opportunity. Deployment and automatic impact measurement are available only for supported opportunity types and configured routes. An opportunity is a recommendation to assess—not a promise of deployment access or measured uplift.
