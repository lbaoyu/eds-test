Where to focus

Four moves to protect a winning position

Canonicalize the UOB domain footprint

The biggest "third-party" citations are UOB's own group domains. Consolidate or set canonical tags so those citations count as owned — an immediate lift with no new content.

Guarantee accuracy on Transactions and Payments

These 21 prompts are the highest-frequency, highest-risk servicing answers. Keep fees, timelines and steps correct and current; a stale answer here has the largest blast radius.

Prerender servicing-critical pages

The compare and payment-modes pages hide most of their content behind client-side rendering. For a FAQ category that is the whole answer.

Add a generic discovery layer

Every tracked prompt is branded. Introduce unbranded card-servicing prompts to measure whether UOB can own the discovery surface too, not just questions that already name UOB.
