Off-site GEO for AI answers

Most of what AI knows about you is built off your domain

On-site GEO makes your own pages quotable. But an AI answer about you is assembled mostly from other sources — Reddit threads, Wikipedia, reviews, news and video. On-site work alone can’t move those. Off-site GEO is how you shape them.

What off-site GEO is

Earning accurate, well-placed representation on the third-party sources AI cites — without buying it. The goal is to be present, correct and current wherever the model looks.

Why it’s different from PR

Classic PR chases impressions. Off-site GEO chases citations: being the source a model quotes. That rewards original data, extractable facts and authentic community presence over reach.

The off-site reality

Where each engine looks

Off-site sources barely overlap between engines, so the same play lands very differently. Match the surface to the engine you’re trying to win.

Concentration

A handful of domains carry the answer pipeline — the top 15 absorb roughly 68% of all citations. Being on them beats being everywhere.

Low overlap

Only ~11% of domains are cited by both ChatGPT and Perplexity, so a single off-site play can’t win all engines.

Volatility

Citation shares move in weeks, not years — one platform change cut ChatGPT’s Reddit share from ~60% to 10% in six weeks.

The one constant

Original, proprietary data is the strongest cross-platform citation lever — the play that pays off on every engine.

01

Original data & first-party research

OwnerPRPMM Best forAll engines

Publish proprietary statistics, benchmarks or studies that don’t exist anywhere else, with a clear method.

Why it moves AI visibility
  • Original data is the single strongest cross-platform predictor of being cited.
  • Editors and AI both prefer a citable number to another opinion.
Research-backedOriginal, proprietary data is the strongest single content signal for citation across engines. — Princeton GEO / 2026 citation analyses
Instead of

Republish the same industry stats every competitor already quotes.

Do this

Run your own study — “In our 2026 analysis of 1,200 exports, median time was 1.8s” — with method and dataset, so you become the source.
