The recommendation

Five accelerators, mapped to the closed loop

Rather than a flat menu, the portfolio is five focused accelerators mapped to the AI visibility closed loop — Discover, Prioritise, Optimise, Deploy, Measure. Three must-haves run the loop end to end; two high-priority accelerators keep it turning for enterprise and live customers.

The loop mirrors the product story: Semrush AIO powers discovery and prioritisation, and Adobe LLM Optimizer optimises, deploys, and measures — then measurement re-seeds the next cycle.

Accelerator 1 opens the loop. Across the middle, the work runs as two parallel lanes that meet at Optimise: a technical lane (Accelerator 2 — implement & deploy) and a content & proof lane (Accelerator 3 — optimise the priority content & measure). They're bought and delivered together, staggered by wave — implementation ships, content and measurement follow, and results re-seed the next wave. Accelerator 5 is a vertical slice driving one or two topics through the full loop repeatedly; Accelerator 4 is the governance ring around all of it.

The five accelerators

From discovery to a self-sustaining optimisation loop

Each accelerator is a short, outcome-driven engagement with a clear owner and defined deliverables. Accelerator 1 opens the loop; the middle runs as two parallel lanes — a technical implementation lane and a content-and-measurement lane, bought and delivered together and staggered by wave; and governance plus recurring sprints keep the loop turning.

01
Must-have Semrush AIO · Discover & Prioritise

AI Visibility Baseline & Prompt Strategy

Primary outcome · Establish the measurement model and the prioritised backlog that feeds the loop.

Best for · Every new customer

What it covers

  • Define the customer's AI visibility goals and success criteria
  • Build the prompt strategy across branded, non-branded, category, comparison, and high-intent prompts
  • Map topic clusters and the competitor set
  • Establish the baseline and rank the opportunity backlog for the Optimise stage

Sample activities

  • Goals and AI-discovery alignment workshop
  • Prompt and topic mapping session
  • Competitor benchmarking in Semrush AIO
  • Baseline review; backlog prioritisation by impact and effort

Deliverables

  • Prompt strategy framework
  • Topic and competitor map
  • Baseline measurement scorecard
  • Prioritised optimisation backlog
  • 30 / 60 / 90-day action plan
Customer value — Faster time to value, meaningful measurement from day one, and a ranked backlog the rest of the loop runs on.
02
Must-have Technical lane · Optimise & Deploy Delivered with Accelerator 3

Technical Implementation & Edge Deployment

Primary outcome · Make the site AI-readable and ship it — technical remediation deployed at origin and edge.

Best for · Customers with web / engineering teams

What it covers

  • Assess technical readiness for AI discovery — crawlability, rendering, and agent access
  • Fix structure, headings, summaries, FAQs, and schema / markup against the prioritised backlog
  • Prepare pages and templates so AI answer surfaces can parse and cite them reliably
  • Deploy improvements at origin and at edge for low-latency, agent-ready delivery

Sample activities

  • Technical readiness and crawlability audit
  • Remediation of structure, markup, and rendering
  • Edge deployment setup and validation
  • Implementation planning with web, SEO, and platform

Deliverables

  • Technical readiness assessment
  • Structured-markup and template fixes by page type
  • Edge deployment configuration and rollout plan
  • Implementation roadmap or sprint plan
  • Risk and dependency summary
Customer value — A site AI answer engines can read and cite, changes that actually ship, and edge delivery that keeps content agent-ready without waiting on release cycles. Owned by web / engineering.
03
Must-have Content & proof lane · Optimise → Measure Delivered with Accelerator 2

Content Optimisation & Value Measurement

Primary outcome · Steer and prove — optimise the priority content, then tie visibility to traffic, engagement, and revenue proxies.

Best for · Teams optimising messaging and proving ROI

What it covers

  • Optimise the priority content from the backlog for AI answer surfaces — coverage, framing, and messaging
  • Define the measurement framework across visibility, traffic, engagement, and outcomes
  • Track agentic and referral traffic from AI surfaces
  • Build an executive scorecard and feed findings back into Discover

Sample activities

  • Content optimisation against the ranked, priority backlog
  • KPI alignment and scorecard design
  • Attribution and outcome mapping
  • Readout prep and QBR narrative

Deliverables

  • Optimised priority content (top of the backlog)
  • Executive scorecard template
  • KPI framework and glossary
  • Reporting cadence and governance plan
  • QBR-ready business impact narrative
Customer value — The messaging that matters is optimised, value shows early, and a measurement loop keeps re-pointing effort at what moves the business. Owned by content / SEO / analytics with the exec sponsor.
04
High priority The governance ring · All loop steps

Operating Model & Governance for AI Discovery

Primary outcome · Keep the loop turning with durable ownership across SEO, content, PR / comms, analytics, and web.

Best for · Enterprise / global customers

What it covers

  • Define ownership across every function the loop touches
  • Clarify who manages prompts, content, deployment, measurement, escalation, and reporting
  • Establish governance for recurring optimisation reviews
  • Support enterprise-scale alignment across regions or brands

Sample activities

  • Cross-functional stakeholder workshop
  • Current-state ownership and workflow review
  • RACI development session
  • Governance and cadence design

Deliverables

  • Cross-functional RACI
  • Governance framework
  • Review cadence and meeting structure
  • Escalation and approval model
  • Recommended operating model
Customer value — Less ambiguity, faster decisions, and a loop that keeps running consistently across enterprise and global programmes.
05
High priority Full loop · One or two topics

Closed-Loop Optimisation Sprints

Primary outcome · Drive one or two chosen topics through the entire loop on a recurring cadence — depth over breadth.

Best for · Customers already live

What it covers

  • Select one or two high-value topics — a product line, a comparison battleground, or a high-intent journey
  • Run the full loop on that slice: discover, prioritise, optimise, deploy, measure, repeat
  • Prioritise the next actions each sprint by impact and effort
  • Coach teams on a repeatable optimisation rhythm after go-live
  • Track progress against agreed KPIs to demonstrate ongoing value

Sample activities

  • Topic selection and slice definition
  • Review findings across visibility, prompt and topic performance, opportunities, and agentic / referral traffic
  • Ship the next optimisations and validate at edge
  • Align owners across SEO, content, analytics, and web
  • Capture outcomes, lessons, and next-sprint priorities

Deliverables

  • Sprint agenda and loop-review framework
  • Prioritised optimisation action plan for the chosen slice
  • Ownership tracker for agreed actions
  • Progress readout against visibility, traffic, and impact KPIs
  • Next-sprint recommendations
Customer value — Continuous progress on the topics that matter most, better use of ABV insights, a repeatable loop instead of one-time setup, and ongoing evidence of progress for stakeholder reviews.

Rationale

Why this structure works

Product-aligned

Mirrors the closed loop

The three must-haves map straight onto the product story — Semrush AIO discovers and prioritises; LLM Optimizer optimises, deploys, and measures — so positioning follows the platform.

Role-aligned

Each names its owner

Every accelerator states who it's for — new customers, web and content teams, ROI-focused leaders, global programmes, and live teams.

Shape

A band, two lanes, and a ring

Discover & Prioritise opens as one band; the middle runs as two parallel lanes — technical implementation, and content-plus-measurement — that ship and prove together; recurring sprints drive a narrow slice through the whole loop; and governance wraps the lot.
