Overview

A joint trial to measure and improve NHB's visibility in AI answers

For the National Heritage Board's Adobe Brand Visibility (ABV) trial, we propose a structured approach: establish a baseline, identify opportunities to improve NHB's visibility in generative AI results, deploy selected optimizations through Deploy on Edge, and measure the outcome against that baseline.

ABV combines Adobe LLM Optimizer with the Semrush AIO workflow. The trial is delivered as a partnership — Adobe provides the methodology, setup guidance, recommendations, analysis, and readout; NHB owns the configurations and changes within its own website, CDN, Google Search Console, security, analytics, and change-management environments.

How we measure

The GEO metrics that matter

GEO success builds from the bottom up. It starts with whether AI systems can access NHB's content at all, then progresses through visibility, accuracy, citations, and referral traffic toward measurable business outcomes. The trial measures across this hierarchy.

Proposed trial objectives

What the trial aims to achieve

  • Understand how NHB and its priority heritage content are represented in generative AI results.
  • Identify gaps in visibility, mentions, citations, accuracy, and content representation.
  • Identify and prioritize content, technical, and edge-optimization opportunities.
  • Deploy selected approved optimizations to AI-agent traffic through Deploy on Edge.
  • Compare post-optimization GEO results with the initial baseline.

Deploy on Edge

Routing AI-agent traffic through the edge optimization path

Deploy on Edge will be included in the trial, subject to completion of the NHB-owned CDN prerequisites. The capability routes supported AI-agent traffic through the edge optimization path while leaving normal human and SEO-bot traffic on the existing delivery path. Adobe can provide configuration guidance and help verify the setup, but NHB must approve and implement the CDN changes.

Adobe Analytics integration during the trial

What measurement is — and isn't — in scope

Adobe Analytics integration is not included in the ABV trial. It is available as part of the paid ABV / LLM Optimizer offer and cannot be connected during the free trial. The trial therefore will not include Adobe Analytics-based measurement of downstream website engagement, conversions, revenue, or other business-impact metrics — those can be considered if NHB proceeds to a paid ABV deployment.

Not in trial

Adobe Analytics measurement of:

Downstream website engagement · conversions · revenue · other business-impact metrics.

The trial can still assess

  • GEO visibility and brand mentions
  • Citation of NHB pages
  • Accuracy and quality of AI-generated representation
  • Relative visibility against comparable organizations or experiences
  • Agentic and referral-traffic insights, provided CDN log forwarding is configured
  • Pre- and post-optimization changes for the agreed prompt and page cohort

CDN log forwarding is an important prerequisite because it supports ABV's agentic and referral-traffic insights, giving traffic-level visibility during the trial. Richer downstream engagement and business-impact analysis would come from Adobe Analytics integration, available in a paid ABV deployment.

Assessing expected improvement

Opportunity and observed change, before and after

Yes — the trial can assess both the opportunity for improvement and the observed change after implementation.

Before changes are made, Adobe can establish a baseline and identify areas where NHB may have an opportunity to improve — for example, where NHB is not mentioned, relevant NHB pages are not cited, comparable heritage organizations are more visible, or the generated representation does not accurately reflect NHB's content.

After NHB implements the agreed changes and Deploy on Edge is active, Adobe can re-run the same core prompt set and compare the results with the baseline.

We recommend treating any expected improvement as directional until the baseline and opportunity assessment are complete. We would not commit to a fixed percentage uplift in advance, because GEO results can vary by AI platform, model, prompt set, market, content changes, and remeasurement timing.
