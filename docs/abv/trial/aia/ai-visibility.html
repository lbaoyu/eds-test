01

What AI Visibility measures

Within Adobe Brand Visibility's Semrush-powered section, AI Visibility is a benchmark score — surfaced as a competitor-relative percentage — that indicates how often, and how prominently, a selected brand appears in AI-generated answers, benchmarked against the other brands competing in the same topics, market, and time period.

"How present is this brand in AI answers, relative to the competitive baseline?"

Because the score is relative, a brand's absolute mention count doesn't map cleanly onto its AI Visibility. It's best read as a positioning signal against the market, not a headcount of appearances.

02

How it's calculated

AI Visibility is a benchmark score on a 0–100 scale, surfaced in Brand Visibility as a competitor-relative percentage. It is not a mention tally — think of it as a function of four ingredients.

AI Visibility = f (    Topic Coverage,      // breadth — how many topics you appear in    Mention Consistency,  // depth   — how reliably you recur in them    Topic Importance,    // weight  — high-demand topics count more    Competitive Normalisation // scale  — to 0–100 vs other brands  )

Breadth

Topic Coverage

Across how many distinct topics your brand appears in AI answers, versus every other brand in scope.

Depth

Mention Consistency

Within those topics, how reliably your brand recurs across the different prompt variations AI is asked.

Weighting

Topic Importance

Topics people ask AI about more often — higher AI Volume — count for more than niche ones.

Scaling

Competitive Normalisation

The blended result is scaled to a 0–100 score relative to the other brands in the same market.

Topic Importance then tilts the result toward where demand actually is. Appearing in a high-volume topic like "best running shoes" moves the score far more than a niche one like "barefoot shoes," so two brands with identical raw coverage won't land in the same place if one owns the topics people actually ask about.

The pipeline, end to end

  • 1Define the topic set
    Around 100 topic clusters per category, with ~10 prompt variations each.
  • 2Collect the responses
    ~1,000 real AI answers captured from actual requests — not the models' APIs.
  • 3Measure coverage & consistency
    How many topics include the brand, and how often it recurs within them.
  • 4Apply topic importance
    Weight higher-volume topics more heavily than niche ones.
  • 5Normalise against competitors
    Scale the blend to a 0–100 score for the selected market and period.
  • 289M+ prompts · 40+ regional datasets

Treat it as a directional benchmark, not a reproducible formula. The weights shown above — the 60/40 split and topic weighting — are illustrative; Semrush publishes the concepts, not the exact math. Brand recognition is context-aware rather than text matching, and AI answers shift constantly, so the score is a reliable directional signal for benchmarking and trends, not a figure to rebuild from raw counts.

Citations ≠ visibility

High citations don't automatically mean high AI Visibility

A brand can be cited often for a narrow set of technical topics while competitors show up across a far broader set of category questions.

That brand might have high cited-page counts and high raw mentions in its strongest topic, yet a lower overall AI Visibility because its presence is less competitive across the full category.

This is why AI Visibility comes with topic, prompt, competitor, and source-level drill-downs — so you never have to rely on the headline score alone.

05

Don't confuse it with these

Three neighbouring metrics get mixed up with AI Visibility. Here's the clean line between them.

AI Visibility vs Brand Visibility

AI Visibility

Uses Semrush's broad, market-level prompt database to set a baseline and compare brands.

Brand Visibility

Measures how frequently your brand appears across your own configured, monitored prompt set.

AI Visibility vs Source Visibility

AI Visibility

Is the brand appearing in AI answers?

Source Visibility

Is the brand's website being cited as a source? A brand can be mentioned without its site being cited — or vice versa.

AI Visibility vs the legacy Visibility Score

AI Visibility

The newer, Semrush-powered market-intelligence metric used for competitor benchmarking and opportunity discovery.

Legacy Visibility Score

Renamed Brand Visibility and simplified to focus on brand mentions when LLM Optimizer evolved into Brand Visibility.
