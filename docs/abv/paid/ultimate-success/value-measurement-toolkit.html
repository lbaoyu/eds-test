This toolkit covers ABV tracked-prompt measurement using Adobe Brand Visibility (ABV). Semrush Suggestions appear only as an optional way to discover candidate prompts. Do not include Semrush market metrics or suggestion-card results in an ABV strategy scorecard or readout.

Read before using the samples

All customer names, prompts, dates, and results below are fictional examples, not benchmarks or expected outcomes.

Strategy definition

What is an ABV Opportunity Workspace Strategy?

ABV Opportunity Workspace Strategy: The initiative-level record in Adobe Brand Visibility's Opportunity Workspace for planning, organizing, and tracking a defined AI-visibility optimization effort. It groups related Workspace Opportunities around a common goal and records the initiative's objective, scope, monitored prompts, progress, and outcomes.

An ABV Opportunity Workspace Strategy is not a general business strategy, a prompt set, or one individual content change. Prompt Strategy is the prompt-discovery experience; it is distinct from an Opportunity Workspace Strategy.
Sources: Opportunity Workspace; Prompt Management

Scope rule: Group opportunities under one evolving strategy when they share an objective, topic/use case, and measurement scope. Split them when those boundaries materially differ—or when separate rollout batches need independent measurement. A strategy-level result does not identify which individual opportunity caused a change.

Before the baseline

When to curate prompts

For an evolving strategy, draft the objective and topic first, then curate prompts before establishing the baseline or starting optimization work. Review Semrush Suggestions if useful, select relevant queries, add them to the ABV Prompt Library, and verify they are available before relying on them for the baseline. Newly added prompts may take up to 24 hours to process.
Sources: Semrush Suggestions; Prompt Management

Recommended sequence

  1. Define the strategy's objective, product/topic, market, and target content.
  2. Review candidate prompts; use Semrush Suggestions as discovery input, if relevant.
  3. Add selected prompts to the ABV Prompt Library and allow time for processing.
  4. Finalize and record the core prompt cohort and version.
  5. Create the evolving ABV Opportunity Workspace Strategy and capture the baseline before related work begins.
  6. Keep the cohort stable for comparisons. If new prompts are added after baseline, identify them as a new cohort/version rather than silently changing the original.

Boundary: Semrush recommendation or market metrics are not ABV strategy outcomes. Keep them out of the ABV scorecard and readout.

Illustrative use case

Sample scenario

Fictional example: Northstar Workforce wants to improve how AI-generated answers represent its employee shift-scheduling capabilities for distributed retail teams in the United States.

The team creates one evolving ABV Opportunity Workspace Strategy with a stable prompt cohort. Three related Workspace Opportunities are tracked beneath it. The strategy-level report shows what moved for the measured strategy scope; it does not attribute results to an individual opportunity.

The sample prompt count and results are illustrative only—not a universal recommendation or benchmark.

Capture consistently

Measurement protocol

Blank protocol
Reusable Markdown template
  1. Agree on the strategy objective, type, product/topic, market, target content, and ABV configuration.
  2. Curate prompts and add selected prompts to the ABV Prompt Library before baseline capture when the workflow uses those prompts.
  3. Verify availability; record the stable core prompt cohort and version.
  4. Capture baseline before related optimization work begins, following the supported workflow.
  5. Add each related Workspace Opportunity to the strategy with an owner, status, and date.
  6. Keep the cohort and configuration stable; record any changes or coverage gaps.
  7. Capture follow-up results using the customer's supported ABV workflow.
  8. Report strategy-level results alongside the opportunity timeline. Don't assign movement to one opportunity without separate evidence.

Three related Workspace Opportunities are added over time, each with an owner and completion date. The final readout reports strategy-level results for the measured scope; opportunity dates provide implementation context, not proof of individual impact.

Filled sample — Northstar

Northstar Workforce — Improve shift-scheduling visibility

The strategy grouped three related Workspace Opportunities: clarifying capability and integration information, adding answers to common scheduling questions, and improving internal links across the scheduling content group. The team recorded each opportunity's owner, status, and completion date.

For the same eight-prompt US-English cohort and recorded ABV configuration, the fictional follow-up values were higher than baseline for Brand Visibility (28 to 33 displayed units) and Source Visibility (12 to 17 displayed units). The team also recorded Responses with Mentions, Responses with Citations, and Total Responses / executions as context.

These are strategy-level results for the measured scope. Because several related opportunities were completed within this strategy, the readout does not claim that any individual opportunity caused the observed movement. The team will review prompt-level evidence and decide whether to continue monitoring this cohort or define a separate strategy for another use case.
