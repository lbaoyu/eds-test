Knowledge sharing & enablement

For: Stakeholders who need the end-to-end operating model without a screen-by-screen execution guide.

After reading, you should be able to:

  • Explain the six stages, accountable roles, and output of a complete GEO loop.
  • Separate strategy coordination, publishing routes, and measurement eligibility.
  • Describe how evidence leads to scale, iterate, hold, or stop.

A loop is a learning cycle, not a feature checklist. Start with one business goal, use a stable prompt cohort, record the baseline, deliver approved work, then review the evidence and decide what happens next. Opportunity Workspace coordinates the initiative; Prompt Strategy helps select conversations, and Prompt Library defines ongoing monitoring.

Frame

Start with the business decision

Choose a focused audience, journey, and visibility gap. Define what should improve and why it matters before choosing prompts or optimizations.

Key question
Which discovery, representation, citation, or engagement gap is worth addressing?
Lead
Business or strategy owner, supported by content/SEO and analytics.
Output
An agreed objective, scope, owner, primary measure, and review criteria.

ABV role: Opportunity Workspace organizes related work around the objective. Keep the business hypothesis separate from a promised result; no universal uplift threshold applies to every loop.

Curate

Select the conversations that matter

Use market and audience evidence to choose a relevant prompt cohort. Include the intents and branded/unbranded questions that reflect the selected journey, then keep the core set identifiable for comparison.

Key question
Do these conversations represent the audience and goal we selected?
Lead
Content/SEO, with the strategy owner confirming relevance.
Output
A processed, stable core cohort with a clear market-language and platform scope.

ABV role: AI Visibility informs market discovery; Prompt Strategy suggests candidates; Prompt Library contains the tracked set. Broad market-discovery scores are not a baseline for your own cohort.

Baseline

Record the starting position before changes

Capture visibility and relevant supporting evidence under an agreed measurement scope. Decide how the follow-up will be compared before implementation starts.

Key question
What evidence will let us judge movement fairly?
Lead
Analytics or measurement lead, supported by the practitioner team.
Output
A dated baseline, exact metric names and units, and comparable review windows.

ABV role: Brand Presence measures the tracked cohort. Brand Claims and URL Inspector add representation and page context; Agentic Traffic shows agent requests, while Referral Traffic shows human visits. Connected analytics may add business outcomes. Preserve a supported experiment's separate baseline and scope.

Plan

Turn the evidence into owned work

Select the changes most relevant to the gap, group related opportunities, and agree responsibility, approval, delivery route, validation, and rollback.

Key question
Which action addresses the gap, and who can deliver it?
Lead
Strategy owner with content, web, IT/CDN, and required reviewers.
Output
An approved action plan with owners and an explicit evaluation approach.

ABV role: Opportunities proposes work; Opportunity Workspace coordinates surfaced and manually created opportunities. An opportunity is a recommendation, not published content. Creating a strategy does not automatically make it an experiment.

Implement

Deliver the approved change and validate it

Use the appropriate publishing route, confirm what actually went live, and preserve implementation dates and scope so the later evaluation remains interpretable.

Key question
Is the approved work live for the intended audience or agent?
Lead
Content, web, IT/CDN, or an offsite owner, depending on the work.
Output
Validated delivery, known guardrails, and an accurate record of what shipped.

Owner-managed publishing

Use CMS, engineering, catalog, or partner processes for work outside the supported edge route. Offsite work needs coordination and confirmation that the change was actually published.

Supported Optimize at Edge

Serve eligible changes through configured CDN routes to AI agents, without changing the origin CMS or human/SEO delivery. Confirm opportunity support, route coverage, and validation controls.

Keep execution and measurement separate. Edge deployment does not guarantee an automatically generated strategy, an Impact Measurement Engine report, or positive uplift.

Review

Interpret the evidence and choose the next move

Compare like with like, review improved and worsened signals, and state the evidence limits. The purpose is a defensible decision—not a requirement to show a positive result.

Ongoing cohort evaluation

Use the same Prompt Library cohort, configuration, and comparable Brand Presence periods. Add relevant claims, URL evidence, referrals, and connected analytics. Evaluate manually when automatic measurement is not supported.

Supported experiment evaluation

Use the experiment's own prompts, measured URLs, dates, and reported outcomes. IME is documented for eligible Recover Content Visibility work; do not generalize that eligibility or its metrics to every optimization. Confirm current support in your account.

Key question
What does the evidence support, and what should we do next?
Lead
Measurement lead and business owner agree the interpretation.
Output
A reviewed readout with results, limitations, and a next action, owner, and date.

Scale

The agreed criteria are met; repeat or expand with a clearly defined next scope.

Iterate

Refine the action or approach while keeping the original comparison identifiable.

Hold

Evidence is insufficient; assign the blocker and next review date. Measurement stays open.

Stop

Continuing is not justified; record the learning and any correction or rollback.

A complete loop connects goal → cohort → baseline → owned work → validated delivery → evidence and a decision. A deployment or UI status alone is not completion. Cohort movement does not prove one action caused it, and AI-referred conversions are not causal revenue attribution. Add parallel loops only when implementation and review capacity are ready.
