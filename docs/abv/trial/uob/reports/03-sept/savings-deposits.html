Where to focus

Five moves that would move the needle

Win "best savings account in Singapore"

The highest-value generic surface in the category, and the one where UOB leads by the thinnest margin. Build a structured, current-rate comparison answer page assistants can quote directly.

Close the citation gap against OCBC and DBS

At 48% owned, half the sourced answers are competitors. Expand authoritative, quotable rate and eligibility content so UOB pages displace OCBC/DBS and the aggregators on generic queries.

Prerender the comparison surface

The compare-accounts tool and FX-rate pages render extra content client-side. Make it crawler-eligible before the next visibility read.

Protect the branded lead

One and Stash are locked up today. Keep their pages current and structured so the 72% / 59% SOV does not erode as rivals publish comparison content.

Keep rate content dated and structured

Savings answers hinge on interest rates; clearly dated, tabular rate content is far more citable than prose and guards against stale-answer risk.
