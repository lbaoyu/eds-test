Executive summary

BDO owns the branded banking answer, but not the category

Across Weeks 32–35, 2026, BDO is the clear leader in AI-generated banking answers for its market: it is named more often and its own pages are cited more often than any tracked competitor. That strength is concentrated in core banking — accounts, digital banking, cards, transfers. The moment a question moves to insurance, life protection, or investing, BDO all but disappears and the models fall back on regulators, reference sites, and competitors.

The one-line read

BDO has won the branded answer and the strongest owned-domain footprint in the market. The growth is no longer about defending accounts — it is about showing up in the unbranded, high-intent questions (protection, investing, comparison) where the buyer is still deciding, and about making sure crawlers can render the content BDO already publishes.

Competitive position

Named and cited more than the field

Over four weeks BDO recorded 234 mentions and 204 citations in tracked answers — roughly 2.0× the mentions and 6× the citations of the nearest Philippine bank. Mentions mean the brand is named; citations mean an owned page was used as a source — the harder, more defensible signal, and the one where BDO's lead is widest.

The visibility gap

Insurance, protection, and investing are open ground

68% of the 90 tracked non-branded prompts return zero BDO visibility. The gap is not evenly spread: core banking prompts average 13% visibility, but insurance and life-insurance prompts average just 2%, with 80% at absolute zero. Across 24 high-interest topics BDO ranks #1 in only three and is entirely absent from sixteen.

These are high-popularity, unbranded, pre-decision questions — exactly where a buyer forms a shortlist. Closing even the top insurance and investing topics would move BDO from three #1 topics toward double digits.

Citation landscape

Rendering & Edge

The crawler can't see most of what BDO publishes

Several high-value pages render almost nothing before JavaScript executes — and AI crawlers largely read the pre-render DOM. bdo.com.ph's homepage exposes 2 words to a crawler versus 2,694 after render: a content gain ratio over 1347×. Content that isn't in the served HTML can't be cited, no matter how good it is.

The fix

Prerendering / server-side rendering these routes — Deploy on Edge for the AEM-served pages — puts the full content in the initial HTML the crawler reads. It is the highest-leverage technical move here: it unlocks pages BDO has already built, no new content required.

Sequencing

Prioritize by traffic × gain ratio: the homepage and /personal hub first (highest gain), then the securities landing page and forex — content-rich routes currently at single-digit content visibility.
