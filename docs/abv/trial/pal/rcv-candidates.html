Recommendation

Two groups, one intervention: recover existing content.

Apply Recover Content Visibility (RCV) to two focused groups of 25 pages each, aligned to Philippine Airlines’ two GEO pilot goals. Every selected URL appears in the supplied RCV opportunity export with measured before-and-after content evidence. No homepages are included.

RCV-only scope. Recover and serve existing rendered page content to supported AI agents through Optimize at Edge. This recommendation does not include new copy, summaries, FAQs, structured-data changes or any other optimization type.

The distinction between the groups is the business outcome: Group 1 strengthens access to authoritative product and service information; Group 2 strengthens access to PAL’s network and destination information. RCV addresses the delivery gap. It does not create missing facts or guarantee how an AI platform will answer.

Group 1 · Brand & Product Representation

Make PAL’s own product and service information readable.

Pilot goal: PAL’s products and services have an accurate AI presence with positive sentiment.

This group prioritizes baggage, cabin, fare, refund, travel-assistance and loyalty information. These are the authoritative pages AI agents need to represent PAL correctly. Several already earn citations or referral traffic despite limited readable content; others address policy questions where the current answer is neutral or incomplete.

Selection rationale: combine clear product/service relevance with a measured RCV gap, giving priority to existing cited sources, observed agent demand and direct policy-question relevance. Recovering restrictions and exceptions matters as much as recovering benefits. No policy or benefit wording will be changed.

Group 2 · Destination & Network Share of Voice

Make PAL’s network and destination content available during planning.

Pilot goal: grow PAL’s discoverability for the routes and destinations it covers during trip planning.

This group combines network and timetable information, route/service announcements, connection information and destination guides. The first 20 pages prioritize international network and planning relevance. The final five broaden destination discovery through existing Philippines travel content.

Selection rationale: recover route-aligned and already-cited sources first, then extend readable coverage to relevant destination content. The broader guides support the wider business goal but are less directly aligned to the current international city-pair prompts; assess their contribution separately rather than attributing all route-pilot movement to them.

Measurement and activation

Keep the pilot focused on recovery and business outcomes.

Before activation: confirm each page contains current approved information and that the recovered version exposes the intended content. Route announcements require a validity check; policies require confirmation of their current conditions. Resolve access or canonical issues separately before activation where needed. A page’s presence in the opportunity export is not production approval.

Confirm the RCV result: verify that the existing primary content is readable in the AI-agent response, with dates, restrictions and qualifications intact. No additional content or other Optimize at Edge changes are proposed.

Measure the two goals separately: use the same prompts, market, AI platforms and competitor set before and after activation. Group 1 maps to 95 product/service prompts; Group 2 maps to 105 discovery/comparison prompts. Track citations to the selected pages alongside the goal-level metrics. Report the broader destination-guide subset separately from the international-route subset.

Interpretation: improved content access is the direct RCV outcome. Citation share, share of voice and sentiment are downstream outcomes to observe after sufficient recrawling and answer-generation activity—not guaranteed uplift. RCV cannot correct inaccurate or outdated source content.

Evidence notes

Selection draws on all 22 supplied files: the HTML report defines the two business goals; the RCV opportunity export establishes page eligibility; URL and weekly agent data, citations, referrals, source comparisons and the prompt inventory inform prioritization. The report’s historical W32–W35 results are not used as current baselines. The newer prompt-insight trends reference W42–W43; some companion exports are undated snapshots.

Words before/after and recovery multiples are observations from the RCV opportunity export, not post-deployment results. Agent requests come from the URL inventory; citations are all-topic URL totals from the owned-URL export, not goal-specific counts. These exports do not establish a single synchronized reporting window. Zero recorded citations means no citations in that export, not absence of potential.

The reference owned-share baselines use the export’s Owned label, which includes some nonproduction sources. A stricter www + flights production-host view is 46.8% for Group 1 and 16.4% for Group 2. Establish an approved production-source allowlist for the pilot readout. Accuracy requires a separate factual review; sentiment is not an accuracy measure.

Only URLs with measured recovery evidence in the supplied RCV opportunity export are included. Adobe guidance on Recover Content Visibility grounds the selection and deployment mechanism.
