Reporting model

Three questions, three sources

ABV’s Brand Presence view is the main reporting surface for tracked-prompt Visibility Score, mentions, citations, sentiment, competitor comparisons and trends. Treat the Visibility Score as a headline indicator and use its component metrics to understand movement; it is not a factual-accuracy score. Accuracy, completeness and freshness require a manual answer review against Australia Post-approved information.

Manual review

Whether the answer is right

Scored on a sample of responses against Australia Post-approved information. These are not ABV dashboard scores.

  • AccuracyClaims match Australia Post-approved information.
  • CompletenessThe answer includes the steps, options and conditions the customer needs.
  • FreshnessDates, deadlines, hours and conditions are current for the place and period.
  • Citation supportEach citation supports the claim it accompanies.

Trial objectives

Where each trial objective is measured

The three objectives from Australia Post’s brief, mapped to the measure that answers each one.

Objective 01

Accuracy of AI responses

Are answers about products, services, and help and support accurate, complete and up to date?

Measured in
Manual reviewABV Brand Claims
OutputIncorrect and missing information, and the content to clarify or strengthen.
  • Score a sample of responses for accuracy, completeness and freshness against Australia Post-approved information, and check that each citation supports its claim.
  • Track owned citation share (owned ÷ all citations in Data Insights) as a leading signal that answers draw on Australia Post content.
  • Use Brand Claims to choose the sample: recurring and unfavorable claims first, plus Contradicted claims where Fact Checking is enabled.
  • Leads the metrics for the four accuracy-led topics.
Objective 02

Sentiment across topics and intents

Is Australia Post described positively, negatively or neutrally, and does that vary by topic, customer need or platform?

Measured in
ABV Brand PresenceABV Brand Claims
OutputWhere portrayal varies by topic, customer need or AI platform.
  • Report sentiment for the tracked prompts; compare by topic, AI platform, and branded versus non-branded prompts.
  • Brand Claims adds claim-level sentiment, showing which statements drive favorable or unfavorable portrayal.
  • The trial has no user-intent filter, so tag topic names by intent at setup (for example, “Tracking · Support” or “Small business · Compare”) to read sentiment by intent.
  • A supporting measure for fast and same-day delivery and the app, and also reported for small-business shipping.
Objective 03

Competitive benchmarking

How are Australia Post’s offerings represented against competitors, and where are others recommended first?

Measured in
ABV Brand Presence
OutputWhere competitors lead, and where content and market presence can be strengthened.
  • Share of Voice, Visibility Score, mentions and citations on non-branded prompts; leads the two visibility-led topics. Branded help prompts would inflate Australia Post’s standing, so keep them out of Share of Voice readouts.
  • Market Tracking compares Australia Post with up to five competitors on the same prompts. AI Visibility adds broader market context but does not substitute for tracked-prompt results.
  • Comparative claims in Brand Claims show how Australia Post is described against alternatives.
  • During manual review, record Australia Post’s position when options are listed.

Brand Claims

Use Brand Claims to target the manual review

Brand Claims analyzes the same Brand Presence responses and extracts the individual statements AI systems make about Australia Post. It shows what is being said; a surfaced claim is not verified as fact.

ABV Brand ClaimsGenerally available

What AI systems say, claim by claim

Built from the responses to the tracked prompts.

  • ClaimsIndividual statements extracted from responses, with similar claims grouped into clusters.
  • SignalsSentiment, category, brand relevance, claim type, recurrence and cited sources.
  • Primary sourcesWhether claims draw on Australia Post’s own site or on external sources.
  • RecommendationsOwned and earned actions per claim, which can be saved as a strategy in ABV’s Opportunity Workspace.
  • Offsite sourcesCited YouTube, Reddit and Wikipedia sources linked to offsite opportunities, when available.
Fact CheckingEarly access

Evidence check on selected claims

Part of Brand Claims. Use it to triage, not to score.

  • VerdictsSupported, Contradicted, Not verified or Not checked.
  • EvidenceAustralia Post’s own content and the open web, not an Australia Post-approved fact set.
  • CoverageOnly claims that also receive recommendations are checked, so many show Not checked.
Limits of Fact Checking
  • Verdicts reflect retrieved evidence only.
  • Not verified does not mean false.
  • Inaccurate claims without retrievable evidence are not flagged.

Confirm early access is enabled for Australia Post’s organization before including it in trial reporting.

What it adds to the trial

  • AccuracyReview recurring and unfavorable claims first, and Contradicted claims where Fact Checking is enabled, instead of a random sample.
  • SentimentClaim-level favorable, neutral and unfavorable detail explains what drives Brand Presence sentiment.
  • CompetitiveComparative claims show how Australia Post is described against alternatives. Visibility versus competitors stays in Brand Presence.
  • Content actionSources and recommendations show which owned pages to clarify and which external sources shape the answer.

Claims come only from tracked-prompt responses, so prompt coverage still matters. Check how claim categories map to the selected topics before reporting claims by topic. The final accuracy call stays with the manual review.

Using the measures

One job per measure

Keep outcome reporting, review targeting, setup checks, market context and manual scoring separate.

  • ABV Brand PresenceOutcome reporting

    Report Visibility Score, mentions, citations, sentiment and competitor comparisons for the same tracked prompts. Compare trends by topic and available AI platform, and read Share of Voice on non-branded prompts.

  • Brand ClaimsReview targeting

    Use recurrence, sentiment and sources to choose which claims the manual review checks first, and route recommendations to the Opportunity Workspace.

  • Fact CheckingTriageEarly access

    Where enabled, use Contradicted claims as a triage list for the manual review. Verdicts are not accuracy scores.

  • Prompt Strategy / Prompt LibrarySetup check

    Confirm the 100 prompts cover the selected topics and the intended customer needs. Prompt coverage is a setup check, not a GEO outcome.

  • AI VisibilityMarket context

    Use broader market and topic insights to inform prompt selection. Don’t substitute this broader view for results on Australia Post’s tracked prompts.

  • Manual answer reviewManual scoring

    Score a sample of responses for accuracy, completeness and freshness, and check whether each citation supports the claim it accompanies.
