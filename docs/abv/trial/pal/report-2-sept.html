Enabler · Content deliverability to AI agents

One pipe caps both goals: agents can't read most of the site

PAL's pages render content client-side, so AI agents that don't execute JavaScript receive a near-empty shell — and a fifth of their requests fail outright. This is why Goal 1 answers stay neutral and Goal 2 citations go to aggregators: the content the model would cite simply isn't in the response it reads.

Prioritized actions

Recommendations, mapped to each goal

Ordered by leverage. The first two move both goals at once; the rest target a specific scorecard. All grounded in Adobe LLM Optimizer guidance.

Enable prerender / Deploy on Edge for the high-traffic set Both goalsP1

Serve prerendered, data-hydrated HTML to AI user-agents via CDN routing, starting with the ~15 URLs carrying the bulk of the 291K agent hits on 10×+ pages. This directly raises Goal 1 owned-citation accuracy (product & FAQ pages) and Goal 2 citation share (route & destination pages). Use the Recover Content Visibility optimization and verify with Fixed Suggestions → View Live.

Grounded in Adobe KB: CDN/edge pre-rendering by user-agent; optimization types & View Live verification.

Clear the 404 / 301 / 403 crawl waste Both goalsP1

Remediate the 18.1% non-200 tail: fix or 301-map dead travel-advisory URLs, collapse redirect chains, and audit the 403s — compare browser vs. bot curl to confirm the WAF isn't challenging legitimate AI agents, then allowlist known crawler UAs. Stabilize the /kr homepage and robots.txt as incidents.

Grounded in Adobe KB: diagnosing AI-agent accessibility behind WAF/Cloudflare via UA comparison.

Win route & destination citation share from aggregators Goal 2P1

Target the 74% of route-prompt citations going to FlightConnections, FlightsFrom and competitor route pages. Make PAL's route & destination pages answer-shaped — clear headings, structured data, direct “best for [route / traveler]” facts — and prerendered, so the model cites PAL on trip-planning queries. Prioritize the “Compare & Choose” topic (14.6% SoV).

Convert neutral product answers to positive Goal 1P2

Half of product/service answers are neutral. Enrich loyalty, fares, cabin and baggage pages with benefit-led, inclusion-forward content (what the traveler gets, framed positively) and ensure it's prerendered so agents read it. Lifts the positive-sentiment share without risking the zero-negative baseline.

Remove staging & beta from the AI index Goal 1P2

Protect representation accuracy: add Disallow rules for staging-aem.* and beta.*, set X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow at the dispatcher/vhost for those hosts, and de-index already-surfaced URLs. Using both controls together is stronger than either alone.

Grounded in Adobe KB: preventing non-prod domains from being crawled/indexed (robots.txt + X-Robots-Tag).
