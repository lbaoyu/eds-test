Trial scope & operating guardrails

What populates in a prompt-and-crawl ABV trial

Australia Post’s candidate list spans two distinct agendas: Consumer Support & Operations (Topics 01, 02, 05, 06) and Commercial & Freight Growth (Topics 03, 04). Because Deploy on Edge and CDN log forwarding are out of scope, every recommended metric below relies strictly on dashboards that populate from weekly prompt execution, Semrush market baselines, and public site crawls.

Core architectural rule: Without CDN logs or Deploy on Edge, tracked prompts become your primary measurement instrument. Content updates ship directly in Australia Post’s CMS, and before/after lift is read on a frozen prompt cohort rather than the automated Impact Measurement Engine.

Active & Populated in Trial

Dashboards to build the readout around

  • Brand Presence (Trial): Weekly Visibility Score, Brand Mentions, Citations, Sentiment, Share of Voice, Market Tracking (up to 5 rivals), and prompt-level Data Insights + full answer Details.
  • Brand Claims: Extracts recurring AI claims, Evidence, Top sources, and claim Sentiment from tracked answers (on-demand run with a 7-day cooldown).
  • URL Inspector (Citation fields): Populates URL, Times cited, Prompts cited in, Categories, and Regions for cited auspost.com.au pages.
  • AI Visibility & Prompt Strategy: Semrush-backed Visibility Overview, Market Comparison, Source opportunities, and persona/Semrush prompt gap suggestions.
  • Opportunities (CMS brief only): Falls back to crawling the top ~200 organic pages on auspost.com.au; use findings to brief manual CMS edits.
Blank or Excluded in This Trial

Capabilities not to promise in scope

  • Agent Traffic & Referral Traffic: Both dashboards remain blank without CDN log forwarding; URL Inspector hit columns (Agentic hits, Referral hits) also stay empty.
  • Analytics Business Impact: Adobe Analytics, CJA, and GA4 connectors for conversion/revenue attribution require a paid ABV license.
  • Impact Measurement Engine (IME): Automated pre/post IME reporting requires an eligible Deploy on Edge experiment; manual CMS edits are measured via cohort tracking instead.
  • Paid Brand Presence & Automated Fact Checking: Trial uses composite Visibility Score (no native User Intent filter or aspect-level sentiment), and automated Fact Checking status labels inside Brand Claims are paid Early Access.
  • Single-domain & weekly bounds: Locked to auspost.com.au (AU) across 5 trial LLMs weekly—not suited for intraday Post Office closure alerts.

Standalone prompt sizing

Right-sizing each topic for tracking, optimization, and measurement

Because Australia Post may shortlist a subset of topics for the live trial, each topic below is sized on a standalone basis (setting the 100-prompt org cap aside). Without automated IME, 20–35 prompts per topic—organized into clusters of 5–6 prompts mapped to 1–3 target CMS URLs—is the practitioner sweet spot: large enough to absorb weekly LLM variance, yet lean enough for sustainable manual accuracy auditing.

Too Small · < 18 Prompts

High weekly noise

A single LLM answer flipping mention or sentiment swings topic scores by 7–10%, masking whether CMS content edits actually moved citations across sub-intents.

Sweet Spot · 20–35 Prompts

Actionable cohort signal

4–6 sub-intent clusters (× 5–6 prompts each) cleanly tie prompt cohorts to target auspost.com.au pages while keeping weekly response verification fast.

Too Large · > 40 Prompts

Paraphrase dilution

Near-duplicate phrasings inflate review effort without uncovering new URL gaps, and limit how many priority topics can fit inside a trial workspace.

Trial execution & measurement workflow

How to run optimization and prove lift without Deploy on Edge

Because manual CMS updates do not trigger the automated Impact Measurement Engine, use a disciplined four-step cohort workflow across the trial window so before/after movement is clean and defensible.

Step 01 · Taxonomy & Views

Encode intent & split Custom Views

Shortlist the priority topics, name sub-topics with [Intent] tags (to solve the missing Trial intent filter), lock the 5 Market Tracking competitors, and save two separate Custom Views: one filtered to Non-Branded Commercial (Topics 03/04) and one for Consumer Support.

Step 02 · Two-Week Baseline

Freeze cohorts & benchmark accuracy

Map every 5–6 prompt cluster to 1–3 target auspost.com.au URLs. Capture two weekly Brand Presence (Trial) runs to smooth normal LLM variance, export the baseline CSV, trigger an initial Brand Claims run, and score 4–5 factual prompts per active topic on a simple Ground-Truth Rubric.

Step 03 · Direct CMS Optimization

Publish structured answers on-site

Combine crawl findings in Opportunities (top ~200 organic pages) with citation/claim gaps from Data Insights → Details. Publish crisp policy tables, holding-window distinctions, MyPost Business tier summaries, and canonical cutoff dates directly in Australia Post’s CMS.

Step 04 · Post-Publish Readout

Compare the locked cohort (Weeks 3–6)

Keep prompt wording, region (AU), and platform scope unchanged. Re-measure 2–4 weeks post-publish across Owned Citation Share, URL Inspector Times Cited, Non-Branded Share of Voice, Sentiment, and Verified Claim Accuracy %.

Shortlisting tip for Saurabh’s refinement

If Australia Post activates three to four priority topics for the initial trial (for example, MyPost Business #04 + Fast & Same-Day #03 for Freight alongside Missed Deliveries & Lockers #02 for Marketing/CX), the standalone prompt budgets above (85–90 prompts total) fit comfortably inside the 100-prompt trial ceiling without sacrificing statistical stability—leaving the remaining topics ready to onboard immediately in a paid subscription.
