Your working scheduleThe suggested 60-day plan is not a statement of trial entitlement. Confirm your end date and enabled features with Adobe; leave enough time for measurement.

Your technical scopeCDN log forwarding and Optimize at Edge are included. Start both during setup so traffic data and deployment routing are ready before the baseline.

Frame · Suggested days 1–5

Agree what you are testing

  1. Choose the scope. One brand, one market, one domain, and one or two customer journeys. List the priority products, URLs, and a short set of relevant competitors.
  2. Write the hypothesis and decision criteria. Pick one primary measure, the improvement that would justify continuing, and guardrails such as brand accuracy and site behavior. Do not assume a guaranteed uplift.
  3. Assign owners and confirm access. Name the pilot lead, content/web owner, measurement owner, CDN/security owner, and approver. Complete the applicable Brands Management settings with your administrator; confirm trial limits and the end date with Adobe.
Example hypothesis: “Making our product eligibility information easier for AI to read will increase citations to the selected pages for our agreed customer questions.”

Keep in your trial record

Scope, hypothesis, primary measure and target, guardrails, owners, trial end date, and the CDN setup plan.

Start both technical workstreams now

CDN log forwarding: configure forwarding for the agreed domain and URLs, then verify that data reaches ABV. Agentic Traffic measures AI-agent requests; Referral Traffic measures human visits from AI citation clicks. Analytics integrations can add engagement or conversion evidence.

Optimize at Edge: begin onboarding and configure CDN routing with your CDN/security owner. Log forwarding and deployment routing are separate tasks; complete and verify both before the baseline. Keep a rollback plan for routing changes.

Move on when: the scope, success criteria, owners, and access are agreed, and both technical workstreams have an owner and completion date.

Discover · Suggested days 6–14

Select the questions that matter

  1. Explore the market. Where enabled, review AI Visibility: Visibility Overview, Prompt Research, and Market Comparison. Note relevant questions, competitor gaps, and cited pages.
  2. Curate, rather than collect. Review available recommendations in Prompt Strategy, alongside real customer questions and search data you can access. Remove duplicates and anything outside the agreed journeys.
  3. Build the Prompt Library. Add the selected prompts, organize them by topic, and check market, language, and branded/unbranded coverage. Wait for processing and confirm results are available before baselining.

Keep in your trial record

A small, reviewed prompt set, why you chose it, and any coverage or access gaps.

Move on when: the prompts reflect your chosen journeys and are ready to measure, CDN logs are flowing, and Optimize at Edge routing is configured and verified. Missing recommendation sources should not block a useful prompt set.

Baseline · Suggested days 15–22

Save the starting point before changing anything

  1. Lock the comparison scope. Save the prompt list/version and lock date, competitors, market, language, platforms, filters, and baseline dates. Plan an equal-length post-change period using the same settings.
  2. Capture trial evidence. In Brand Presence (Trial), record Visibility Score, Brand Mentions, and Citations as available. Save the export or dated screenshots. Paid Brand Presence is not required; do not mix different metric definitions in one comparison.
  3. Choose one change. Review relevant Opportunities, Agentic Traffic, and Referral Traffic; add Brand Claims and URL Inspector findings where available. Record the traffic baseline dates and confirm coverage for the target URLs. Select a fix tied to the hypothesis; record its target URLs/prompts, owner, approval, and measurement period.

Keep in your trial record

A dated baseline plus a one-change brief: what, where, why, who, and how you will measure it.

Move on when: visibility and traffic baselines are saved, Optimize at Edge readiness is verified, and the change is approved. Investigate missing expected traffic data before release; mark unavailable data as “not available”—never as zero.

Optimize · Suggested days 23–50

Ship one change and give it time

  1. Deploy one supported optimization. Select an eligible ABV opportunity tied to your hypothesis and deploy it through Optimize at Edge. Use Recover Content Visibility when a relevant AI-readability gap exists; confirm suitable URLs and deployment limits with Adobe. Do not force an unrelated fix just to complete a deployment.
  2. Validate and record the release. Preview the change, verify routing and delivery to AI agents, and check brand accuracy and human/SEO-bot delivery. Record exactly what changed, when it went live, who approved it, and how to reverse it. Use Opportunity Workspace where enabled, or the shared trial record.
  3. Observe the agreed period. Keep the tracked prompt set and comparison settings stable; avoid unrelated changes to target pages. Monitor the primary measure, citations, agentic/referral traffic, and guardrails, noting outages, campaigns, or other changes that could affect interpretation.

Keep in your trial record

A validated live change, deployment date, rollback plan, and post-change evidence.

RCV delivery and impact measurement

Use Recover Content Visibility (RCV) only when a relevant content-readability gap exists. Confirm enabled deployment limits, suitable URLs, CDN routing, and approvals with Adobe. Preview and validate delivery to AI agents; correctly configured routing is intended to leave human and SEO-bot delivery unchanged. Retain a rollback path.

Impact Measurement Engine: confirm eligibility before relying on an automated report. Supported RCV studies typically take about two weeks and use separate URL-specific measurement prompts, not your tracked Prompt Library. URL/data requirements and trial deployment limits can restrict eligibility. Otherwise use the agreed manual comparison; allow more time or report an inconclusive result when data is insufficient.

Move on when: the change is validated and the measurement period is complete—or the remaining data/time limitation is explicitly recorded.

Decide · Suggested days 51–60

Compare like for like. Make the call.

  1. Repeat the baseline readout. Use the same prompts, market, language, platforms, competitors, and filters over the planned comparable period. Recheck affected claims, pages, and citations.
  2. Separate evidence from inference. Compare the primary measure and guardrails. Add agentic/referral traffic or business outcomes only where reliable data exists. A visibility change is not proof of revenue or proof that your change caused it.
  3. Choose the next action. Explain what changed, what remains uncertain, and whether the process is repeatable. Agree a recommendation, next owner, and next date with Adobe before the trial ends.

Keep in your trial record

A short before/after scorecard and one recommendation with evidence, limits, and a named next owner.
