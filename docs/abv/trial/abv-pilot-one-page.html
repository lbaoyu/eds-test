The opportunity

A focused, customer-owned pilot

Sixty days to move from an unknown — how your brand shows up in AI-powered answers — to a defensible baseline, one proven improvement, and a repeatable operating model with a clear next step.

AI assistants and AI-powered search increasingly decide which brands get seen, cited, and recommended. This pilot makes that visibility measurable for your organization, then proves you can move it — on your own content, with Adobe alongside your team.

Pilot principle

Discover the opportunity, define what matters, activate one meaningful improvement, and prove the outcome — before you commit to scale.

Powered by Adobe Brand Visibility — Adobe LLM Optimizer and Semrush AI visibility, working together. Exact capabilities depend on your entitlement and are confirmed with you during Frame.

The finish line

What you’ll have on Day 60

The pilot succeeds when you hold each of these — a defensible read on where you stand, and a clear call on what to do next.

  • An approved, defensible baseline you keep
  • One completed and measured optimization cycle
  • A clear read on what improved and why
  • Visibility into available traffic or business impact
  • A documented, customer-owned operating process
  • A decision-ready recommendation: scale, iterate, hold, or stop

Working together

What we need — and how to start

The pilot is yours; Adobe runs alongside it. A small, senior working group and a fixed cadence keep sixty days on track.

From you

  • A named executive sponsor and a working owner.
  • Owners for content, web, analytics, and approvals.
  • Access to the domains, content, and data in scope.
  • Capacity to ship one change during Activate & Measure.

From Adobe

  • The Adobe Brand Visibility toolset and the pilot method.
  • Market, competitive, and citation analysis.
  • Baseline and measurement setup, and the locked baseline.
  • The Day 60 readout and scale recommendation.

Getting started

To open Frame we need three things: a named sponsor, the one brand and market in scope, and a 45-minute kickoff. The decision date is fixed at the start — the sixty days do not drift.
