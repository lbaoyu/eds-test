Where to focus

Five moves that would move the needle

Build the comparison answer surface

The weakest prompts are all non-branded "best / which / vs." questions. Publish comparison-grade pages (fees, USD/multi-currency, digital business banking) that answer them directly — where SOV and owned citations both leak today.

Defend Business Account Basics

The only topic where UOB is not #1 (rank 3, 15% SOV). OCBC and DBS own the "what is a business account" layer. Reinforce foundational, entity-clear content to reclaim it.

Answer the fintech challengers

Airwallex, Aspire and WorldFirst are cited on multi-currency and SME-fee prompts. Publish quotable, current comparison content on those exact questions.

Convert mentions into citations

DBS and OCBC out-mention UOB on generic queries market-wide, yet UOB already wins citations. Expanding authoritative content on those queries should flip mentions in UOB's favour.

Close the render gap on business pages

Prerender the SME Hub and Structured Trade pages so the full rendered content is crawler- and assistant-eligible.
