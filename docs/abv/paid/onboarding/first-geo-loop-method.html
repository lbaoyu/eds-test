Your first GEO loop:
the method

Six steps to turn a priority visibility gap into implemented work, measured evidence, and a clear next decision.

Assumes ABV access, brand configuration, required data connections, and deployment readiness are already in place.

Frame the strategy

Define the goal before choosing prompts

ABV features

Opportunity Workspace
Plan the parent strategy and its scope.
  1. Choose one priority gap. State the audience, business objective, and what should improve: brand representation, source citations, or content accessible to AI agents.
  2. Set the scope. Identify the brand, target URL or deployment batch, topic, market-language, platform, and exclusions. Keep a first manual initiative focused; group only actions serving the same objective and scope.
  3. Agree ownership and measurement. Name the strategy owner and reviewer, primary metric, evaluation period, guardrails, and criteria for the next decision. Choose the execution route—Optimize at Edge or owner-managed publishing—separately from manual or supported automatic measurement.

Define the brief first. Reuse an existing strategy when its objective and scope match, use a generated strategy when provided—documented for RCV—or create one manually when needed. Do not assume every edge deployment creates a strategy or experiment.

Move on when

The objective, scope, owner, and measurement approach are agreed.

Curate the tracked cohort

Select conversations that match the strategy

ABV features

AI Visibility
Discover market and competitor gaps.
Prompt Strategy
Review recommendations and select prompts.
Prompt Library
Organize the tracked core cohort.
  1. Use discovery to inform selection. Review relevant AI Visibility findings and Prompt Strategy suggestions from Semrush, Google Search Console, Synthetic Personas, or Citation Attempt, as applicable.
  2. Define the tracked prompt set. Build a relevant, deduplicated cohort in the paid Prompt Library, covering important intents and branded/unbranded questions.
  3. Keep a stable core cohort. Maintain consistent wording, library organization, and market-language scope; keep exploratory additions separate. The cohort must be processed and identifiable for comparison; new prompts can take up to 24 hours.

Market-discovery figures are not tracked-cohort results. Paid Brand Presence is also Semrush-powered; its monitored-prompt metrics remain valid. Supported experiment prompts are separate from the Prompt Library.

Move on when

The strategy-aligned cohort is processed, identifiable, and ready for comparison.

Establish the starting position

Capture the baseline before related changes

ABV features

Brand Presence
Baseline monitored-prompt performance.
Brand Claims / URL Inspector
Inspect relevant claims and page evidence.
Agentic Traffic / Referral Traffic · when relevant
Add supporting traffic context.
  1. Record the measurement scope. Use the intended cohort, brand, market-language, platform, filters, comparison brands, dates, and timezone. Do not label an account-wide metric a strategy-specific result if the view cannot isolate your scope.
  2. Capture paid Brand Presence metrics. Use Brand Visibility and Source Visibility with their displayed units. Include Share of Voice only for an agreed competitive objective; response volume is coverage context, not an outcome.
  3. Confirm the comparison protocol. Agree comparable baseline/follow-up windows. Include relevant prompt answers, claims, URL, or traffic evidence. Where automatic measurement is supported and eligible, preserve its pre-deployment baseline and evaluation period as a separate lane; otherwise evaluate manually.

Move on when

The starting evidence and comparison method are agreed before deployment.

Organize the work

Turn the gap into an owned action plan

ABV features

Opportunities
Prioritize changes that address the gap.
Opportunity Workspace
Group surfaced and manually created opportunities; assign owners.
  1. Prioritize what to change. Use the baseline to review ABV's automatically surfaced suggestions and your team's own findings. Agree the proposed change, target URL or claim, and how it addresses the strategy's content or technical gap.
  2. Group the work in Opportunity Workspace. Link existing opportunities or create manual ones within an existing strategy. Group only work sharing the same objective and measurement scope.
  3. Confirm execution, ownership, and approval. Choose Optimize at Edge for supported types, or owner-managed publishing for other onsite/offsite work. Agree owners, approval, timing, validation, and rollback. Check measurement separately; manual work does not automatically join an existing experiment's measured scope.

Move on when

The selected opportunities are in scope, approved, owned, and evaluable.

Implement and validate

Deploy the approved work and verify it is live

ABV features

Opportunities
Review surfaced suggestions for the selected type.
Optimize at Edge · supported types
Deploy and manage supported edge optimizations.
Opportunity Workspace
Track the generated or manually managed strategy.
  1. Use the right delivery route. Deploy supported opportunities with Optimize at Edge; use the responsible CMS, engineering, catalog, or partner owner for other onsite/offsite work.
  2. Confirm the work is live. Validate the intended content and agent experience, resolve failed or partial delivery, and ensure Workspace reflects what shipped. A proposed offsite change is not published work.
  3. Protect the evaluation. Keep scope and implementation dates clear. Monitor guardrails and account for other changes or rollbacks that could affect measurement.

Optimize at Edge serves supported changes to agentic traffic without changing the origin CMS or human/SEO-bot experience; validate the intended delivery. Edge deployment alone does not guarantee automatic impact measurement.

Move on when

The work is live and validated, and Workspace reflects what actually shipped.

Measure and complete

Review the evidence and decide what happens next

ABV features

Brand Presence
Compare the ongoing tracked cohort.
Opportunity Workspace
Review progress and supported outcomes.
Impact Measurement Engine
Use for supported, eligible experiments.
Measurement laneUse the correct scope and metrics
Ongoing Brand PresenceCompare the same Prompt Library cohort, configuration, and comparable periods. Report Brand Visibility, Source Visibility, and selected diagnostics. Use manual evaluation for owner-managed work and edge deployments without automatic impact measurement.
Supported Workspace experimentUse the actual analysis, dedicated prompts, displayed metrics, dates, and measured URL subset. The documented RCV analysis reports Citation Rate and Content Visibility and typically takes about two weeks; do not assume those metrics or timing for every edge opportunity.
  1. Compare accurately. Use exact metric names and units; distinguish percentage-point changes from relative percentages. Keep Workspace scores and supported experiment results separate from Brand Presence. Impact Engine measurement prompts do not count toward the Prompt quota and retire after the experiment.
  2. State the result and its limits. Review improved, unchanged, and worsened evidence. Disclose coverage gaps, changed settings, and other interventions. Report strategy-level movement; do not assign it to one child action or claim revenue causation without separate evidence.
  3. Close with a reviewed decision. Summarize objective and scope → work completed → results → limitations → next action. Have the owner and measurement reviewer agree the conclusion, then update Workspace progress as supported.

Scale

Agreed criteria are met. Repeat or expand through a clearly defined next scope.

Iterate

Refine the action, scope, or method. Keep the original comparison identifiable.

Hold

Evidence is insufficient. Assign the blocker and a next review date; keep measurement open.

Stop

Continuing is not justified. Capture the learning and any rollback or correction.

Your first strategy is complete when…

The approved work is implemented and validated, the outcome has been evaluated with explicit limitations, and a reviewed next decision has an owner and date. A deployed change or UI status alone is not completion; positive uplift is not required. A hold pending evidence does not mean measurement is complete.
