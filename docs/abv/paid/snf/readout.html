Heritage SG & the Singapore Night Festival in AI answers

Across the two weeks to Sep 7, Heritage SG led every tracked competitor in AI answers about the Singapore Night Festival — and AI-referred visits surged ~10× across the window. Yet roughly two in five AI requests to the site were refused, the highest-demand discovery questions returned almost no brand mention, and AI more often cites other people’s sites than heritage.sg.

What stands out

  • Durable, not spiky. Heritage SG holds ~53% across the window; competitors never break the low teens.
  • i Light punches above its weight on citations. It earns 496 citations from just 223 mentions (~2.2× per mention) vs Heritage SG’s ~0.8× — a sign its sources are more citation-ready.
  • The lead is on branded queries. On high-demand unbranded “things to do at night” prompts the brand is near-invisible — that top-of-funnel share is ceded to general listings sites.

03 The core findings

Where the brand wins, where it’s losing ground

Four results define the current GEO picture — each with the evidence beside it.

Visibility & discovery

#1 on branded festival queries — near-invisible on “things to do at night”

Where the brand appears, it ranks first (avg position 1.47). But 14 high-demand, unbranded discovery questions return almost no mention — the top-of-funnel “what should I do tonight” moment is being handed to general listings sites.

Also under-served: evening logistics prompts (getting to Bras Basah / Fort Canning after dark) draw only 1–2 mentions despite high demand — navigational intent the official site should own outright.

AI crawler access

38% of AI crawlers are blocked before they read a thing

2,835 of 7,482 agent requests were refused with a 403; add 13% redirects and only ~60% reach content cleanly. This is the single biggest constraint on citations — page quality is irrelevant if the crawler never gets in. Chatbots (led by ChatGPT) drive three-quarters of all agent traffic.

Smoking gun: the /sgnightfest/programmes hub drew 354 citation attempts but was cited just 7 times (2.0%); even robots.txt succeeds only ~50% of the time.

Citations & sources

AI cites other sites about you — even a competitor’s

heritage.sg is only the #7 most-cited source on its own topic. Government, tourism and media domains sit ahead of it — and so does the competitor domain ilightsingapore.gov.sg (42× vs your 39×). Just 46% of owned-page citation attempts convert. Third parties are narrating the Night Festival story.

Nuance: the top source, nhb.gov.sg (107×), is Heritage SG’s own parent body — a friendly citation, not lost ground. The top-10 domains carry 44% of all citations, so a few wins move the needle.

Referral & representation

AI-referred visits just surged ~10× — presence is converting

AI-referred pageviews jumped from 15 to 156 week over week (+940%, ~10×) — attention arriving straight from an AI answer, almost entirely via ChatGPT (158 of 171 pageviews). Sentiment stays healthy at 59% positive, 0.4% negative. Volume is still small in absolute terms and revenue reads $0 — but the festival is free, so the real KPI is engaged attention, and the top AI-landing pages already show real dwell (a minute-plus), not bounces.

Where sentiment is weakest it clusters on practical logistics — parking, show times, ticket cost and dates (the prompts shown) — reading as neutral or uncertain rather than critical, not hostile.
