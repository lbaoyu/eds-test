The recommendation

Four accelerators for Brand Visibility

The portfolio is four Ultimate Success accelerators, scoped for Brand Visibility — Use Case Mapping to Solution Capability, Value Measurement Framework, Operating Model & Change Management, and Tool Workflow & Governance Optimization — names customers and delivery teams already work with.

Because ABV is one unified product with capabilities powered by both the Semrush platform and Adobe LLM Optimizer, the foundational accelerator — Use Case Mapping — ships in two engine-oriented versions: one that maps goals to the Semrush-powered visibility and prompt capabilities, and one that maps them to the Adobe LLM Optimizer optimisation and technical capabilities.

ABV has no go-live event — provisioning and any one-time technical setup (CDN log forwarding, edge routing) are handled during onboarding, not as a launch milestone. The accelerators pick up after onboarding, with Tool Workflow & Governance Optimization providing the repeatable operating rhythm that keeps optimisation moving.

The four accelerators

Each accelerator, scoped for Brand Visibility

Each is an Ultimate Success accelerator with ABV-specific scope, a clear owner, and defined deliverables. Use Case Mapping is foundational and ships in two engine-oriented versions; Value Measurement proves impact; and Operating Model & Change Management plus Tool Workflow & Governance Optimization keep the motion durable and repeatable after onboarding.

01
Foundational · Plan → Activate Two engine-oriented versions

Use Case Mapping to Solution Capability

Primary outcome · Map the customer's goals to ABV capabilities across both engines — the Semrush-powered market and prompt intelligence, and Adobe LLM Optimizer's optimisation and technical readiness — and turn that into the prioritised plan the rest of the motion runs on.

Best for · Every ABV customer, post-onboarding

Delivered in two engine-oriented versions — run either or both, depending on where the customer needs to focus. For the Semrush-powered version, involve a Semrush SME to fine-tune scope and positioning.

Version A · Semrush AIO oriented

AI Visibility Baseline & Prompt Strategy

Semrush-powered discovery & prioritisation

What it covers
  • Define AI visibility goals and success criteria
  • Build the prompt strategy across branded, non-branded, category, comparison, and high-intent prompts
  • Map topic clusters and the competitor set
  • Establish the AI visibility baseline and Share of Voice
Deliverables
  • Prompt / topic and competitor framework
  • Baseline visibility scorecard
  • Prioritised optimisation backlog
  • KPI shortlist and 30 / 60 / 90-day plan
Version B · Adobe LLMO oriented

Optimisation & Technical Capability Mapping

Adobe LLM Optimizer optimisation & technical readiness

What it covers
  • Map priority use cases to Adobe LLM Optimizer capabilities
  • Identify the content optimisation opportunities LLM Optimizer surfaces
  • Assess technical / structural GEO readiness — crawlability, rendering, schema, agent access
  • Confirm edge-readiness (Optimize at Edge) established during onboarding
Deliverables
  • Use-case → LLM Optimizer capability map
  • Technical GEO readiness summary
  • Prioritised optimisation opportunities
  • Edge-readiness confirmation and next actions
ScopeAdvisory and diagnostic across both versions — builds on onboarding rather than repeating setup. Maps goals to capability and recommends priorities, but does not author or modify content, make code or configuration changes, or remediate technical issues. Prompt strategy stays tied to business objectives rather than an exhaustive library; technical and content readiness is assessed and recommended, not fixed.
02
Attain value · Measure Two measurement lenses

Value Measurement Framework

Primary outcome · A repeatable model that proves the value of each optimisation, then confirms it at market scale over time.

Best for · Teams proving ROI to leadership

Measurement runs through two lenses, read as layers. The LLM Optimizer lens proves the value first — a re-measurement of your curated set against the same baseline and prompts. The Semrush lens then confirms the same gains at market scale over time. They measure different universes, so read them as complementary layers, not comparable scores.

Layer 1 · LLM Optimizer lens

Prove the value

Operational scoreboard · re-measure your curated set · weekly / monthly

What it measures
  • Re-measures the curated tracked-prompt set against the same baseline and prompts
  • Shows the before-and-after delta from each optimisation on priority prompts
  • Tracks visibility, citations, sentiment, and answer quality on the tracked set
  • Ties movements back to the specific changes that drove them
Deliverables
  • Before / after visibility scorecard on the tracked set
  • Per-optimisation impact readout
  • KPI framework, glossary, and weekly / monthly cadence
  • QBR-ready, value-proven impact narrative
Layer 2 · Semrush lens

Confirm it over time

Strategic market lens · full prompt database · quarterly

What it measures
  • Measures visibility against the full market prompt database
  • Benchmarks competitive position at market scale
  • Confirms the same gains surface in the broader market over time
  • Surfaces new market-wide opportunities for the next cycle
Deliverables
  • Market-wide AI visibility trend
  • Competitive benchmark summary
  • Quarterly market-movement readout
  • Inputs that re-seed the next Discover cycle
Customer value — Early, defensible proof of impact on the work you did, plus market-scale confirmation that the gains hold over time.
ScopeMeasure and recommend, not produce — identifies and recommends content changes but does not author, rewrite, publish, or deploy. Business attribution only where supporting data or integrations exist; avoid presenting correlation as proven causation.
03
Optimise & scale · Organisation

Operating Model & Change Management

Primary outcome · Durable cross-functional ownership and change readiness so AI visibility becomes an enterprise capability, not a project.

Best for · Enterprise / global programmes

What it covers

  • Assess current ownership and governance maturity
  • Define roles and decision rights across SEO / GEO, Content, Brand / PR, Analytics, Web, and leadership
  • Address regional and brand scaling considerations
  • Introduce change management and communication to sustain adoption

Sample activities

  • Cross-functional stakeholder workshop
  • Current-state ownership and workflow review
  • RACI development session
  • Change and communication planning

Deliverables

  • Current-state assessment and future-state operating model
  • Cross-functional RACI
  • Governance framework and review cadence
  • Escalation / approval model
  • Change and adoption plan
Customer value — Less ambiguity, faster decisions, and an operating model that scales consistently across regions and brands.
ScopeAdvisory organisational design — does not execute organisational changes, assign customer personnel, or act as the customer's GEO operating team. Leadership retains ownership of organisational decisions and change execution.
04
Activate → Adopt · Operating rhythm

Tool Workflow & Governance Optimization

Primary outcome · A repeatable way to turn ABV insights into action — coordinating the workflows, integrations, and review cadences that keep optimisation moving.

Best for · Live customers operationalising ABV

What it covers

  • Establish the optimisation rhythm — closed-loop sprints on selected high-value topics or use cases
  • Review AI visibility, prompt / topic performance, citations, and competitive gaps; prioritise, review progress vs KPIs, and capture lessons
  • Define how ABV insights flow into connected systems — AEM, Adobe Analytics, Google Analytics, Customer Journey Analytics, and Google Search Console
  • Clarify ownership and handoffs across content, SEO, analytics, and execution teams; establish governance, permissions, and review cadences

Sample activities

  • Topic / use-case selection and sprint scoping
  • Loop review across visibility, prompts, citations, gaps, and agentic / referral traffic
  • Workflow and integration mapping (ABV ↔ AEM / Analytics / CJA / GSC)
  • Ownership, cadence, and governance design

Deliverables

  • Optimisation sprint framework and readouts
  • Prioritised action plan for selected topics / use cases
  • Workflow and integration map across connected systems
  • Ownership / action tracker and review cadence
  • Governance and permissions recommendations; next-cycle priorities
Customer value — A repeatable operating rhythm instead of one-time setup — ABV insights consistently reach the teams and systems that act on them, with ongoing evidence of progress.
ScopeAdobe guides, facilitates reviews, and helps prioritise; the customer or its implementation partner carries out the content, code, deployment, and operational changes. Scope stays focused (e.g. 1–2 topics or use cases per cycle) rather than open-ended managed optimisation.
