The 90-minute agenda

Elapsed time · Select a row for detail

  1. 0–10 minIntroductions and enablement outcomesDiscuss

    Align on the Adobe team's role, the shared customer case, and the outcomes colleagues should be able to explain and facilitate.

  2. 10–25 minAI discovery and ABVLearn

    Practice explaining how GEO complements SEO and distinguish Semrush-powered AI Visibility market research from results for a customer's tracked prompts. Introduce Brand Visibility, Source Visibility, and citations; check each metric's definition in its dashboard. Position answer accuracy as a customer fact-check, not an ABV correctness score.

  3. 25–40 minThe ABV workflowDemo

    Trace AI Visibility research into Prompt Strategy and the Prompt Library, then Brand Presence, Opportunities, optimization, and measurement. Show how selected prompts become the monitoring set.

  4. 40–60 minCustomer readiness and prerequisitesLearn

    Practice discovery across the customer's brand and site domains, aliases, markets, competitors, categories, audience, language, and topics. Qualify access, content ownership, CDN log forwarding for Agentic and Referral Traffic, and analytics integrations for Business Impact; identify what needs follow-up rather than assuming readiness.

  5. 60–80 minReadiness and prompt workshopApply

    Build a customer readiness brief with the use case, prompt sample, owners, and success criteria. Identify prompt categories, market and language coverage, and branded/unbranded balance. Define the baseline and who will capture it before any changes are made.

  6. 80–90 minReview and Q&ADiscuss

    Resolve readiness gaps and assign preparation for Session 2.

Team output

Customer readiness briefObjective, scope, prompts, owners, prerequisites, baseline status, and success criteria.

Between sessions

Use the brief to identify follow-ups for the account team. Capture the baseline if available; otherwise work from the clearly labeled training dataset.

The 90-minute agenda

Elapsed time · Select a row for detail

  1. 0–10 minCustomer context reviewDiscuss

    Review the customer objective, readiness brief, prompt sample, and baseline status. Identify assumptions to validate with the customer.

  2. 10–30 minRead AI Visibility and prompt evidenceLearn

    Explore Visibility Overview, Prompt Research, and Market Comparison, then compare them with the monitored Prompt Library and Brand Presence. Define Brand Visibility, Source Visibility, Share of Voice, and Topic Volume in context; do not compare similarly named metrics or old and current definitions as if they were equivalent.

  3. 30–50 minGuided customer investigationDemo

    Model how to compare brand and competitor presence, inspect relevant answers, trace cited sources, and explain what the evidence does and does not establish.

  4. 50–70 minClaims and opportunity typesApply

    Practice explaining Brand Claims: inspect recurring assertions, sentiment, and sources; validate claims with customer-approved facts because ABV does not independently verify them. Classify opportunities as on-page content, technical GEO, or offsite, and identify the right customer owner or third-party dependency.

  5. 70–85 minPrioritize and frame a recommendationApply

    Rank by customer business relevance, evidence, feasibility, and potential impact. Select three opportunities and practice explaining the first recommendation, its rationale, and what must be validated.

  6. 85–90 minAccount-team handoff and Q&ADiscuss

    Capture the priority hypothesis, customer decision-maker, affected page, proposed change, expected signal, and open dependencies.

Team output

Customer opportunity backlogThree ranked opportunities and one recommendation with evidence, rationale, and dependencies.

Between sessions

Prepare to walk the customer through the target page, factual sources, content owner, and approval or publishing dependencies.

Elapsed time · Select a row for detail

  1. 0–5 minOpportunity recapDiscuss

    Confirm the priority hypothesis, evidence, and target page.

  4. 45–65 minSet customer measurement expectationsLearn

    Distinguish the Impact Measurement Engine's before/after evaluation for supported opportunity types from Referral Traffic's Business Impact reporting.   5. 65–80 minBuild the value scorecardApply

    Use a simulated training dataset to prepare a customer-facing readout: summarize changes, interpret the evidence, and recommend a next action. Label simulated results; do not present them as customer impact or as data entered into ABV.

    Share a short insight-to-impact readout and agree the next optimization cycle.

Use the practice materials to support a customer discussion. Confirm real access, approvals, deployment ownership, and measurement eligibility in the customer's environment.

Learning design and assessment

Practice one connected customer engagement

Build a reusable internal enablement pack across the course: customer readiness brief → opportunity backlog → customer-ready recommendation → value scorecard.

Internal account-team scenario

An Adobe account team is preparing to guide a bank that wants to improve its presence in AI answers about travel credit cards in a priority market.

Practice discovery, interpret the evidence, identify customer stakeholders and dependencies, shape a recommendation, and agree how success could be evaluated. Adapt the case to the account's industry, market, and operating model.

Delivery rhythm

Learn → Demo → Apply → Discuss

Explain the customer decision, demonstrate the workflow, practice the account-team conversation, and review the evidence. Space sessions roughly one week apart for preparation.

Capstone assessment

Assess judgment, not feature recall

Each participant explains how they would guide an account team through the priority, recommendation, owners, and measurement plan. Assess customer alignment, evidence, feasibility, role clarity, and honest interpretation.

Keep three measurement layers separate

01 · What we did

Delivery

Change, owner, approval, and deployment date. A proposed change is not a deployed result.

02 · What became visible

Visibility

Brand Visibility, Source Visibility, citations, and claims checked against approved facts. Use the dashboard's metric definition and consistent scope.

03 · What users did

Business outcomes

Human AI-referral visits, engagement, and conversion where tracked. AI-agent requests are not customer visits.

Define success early. Interpret results honestly.

Capture the baseline before changes and log each deployment. Live results may take longer than the course; before/after movement alone does not prove causation. Use a clearly labeled simulated dataset for the final exercise.

Facilitator preparation

Prepare the internal enablement workbook, demo environment, prompt sample, target page, customer conversation prompts, and clearly labeled simulated before/after dataset. Confirm which reports and features are available in the demo tenant, including Brand Claims and Opportunity Workspace. Explain CDN log forwarding, analytics integrations, and deployment prerequisites as customer-environment checks; treat Optimize at Edge as eligibility-dependent. Supporting materials are separate from this outline.
