The recommendation

One consistent method, differentiated by tier

Keep the 60-day ABV Pilot methodology consistent; differentiate the level of Adobe involvement and coordination based on the customer's Success tier. The method stays fixed — what scales with the tier is how much Adobe frames, aligns, and follows through.
