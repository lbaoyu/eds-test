Learn the method

GEO loop & strategy

A repeatable method that connects business intent, a stable measurement scope, and an evidence-based decision.

  1. Frame

    Set the strategy

    Understand the market, audience needs, and competitors. Choose a business objective and priority visibility gap; agree on scope, owner, and measurement.

  2. Curate

    Build the tracked cohort

    Use market research, search-query evidence, and relevant recommendations. Refine, deduplicate, and organize a stable set of prompts aligned to the objective.

  3. Baseline

    Record the starting point

    Before related changes, capture the brand, prompt cohort, market, data source, and observation window. Verify coverage and record starting evidence.

  4. Plan

    Create owned actions

    Diagnose gaps and prioritize supported opportunities. Assign owners and approvals, confirm technical prerequisites, and choose Optimize at Edge (OaE) or customer publishing.

  5. Implement

    Deploy & validate

    Implement the agreed changes and verify what went live. Confirm rollback readiness; for OaE, check eligible AI delivery and unaffected human / SEO delivery.

  6. Review

    Evaluate & decide

    Compare results using the agreed measurement method. State limitations; decide what to scale, iterate, hold, or stop, with an owner and review date.

Outcome: The team can connect strategy, implementation, and review in one repeatable method.

Guided customer execution

Run the first GEO loop

Start with one focused customer initiative. Use the method above in working sessions, with named delivery owners and a review booked against the agreed observation window.

Delivery lead: Customer GEO lead. Implementers: Content / publishing and IT / CDN owners. Review: Analytics and business owner.

Engagement rhythm

Make it a working engagement

Launch workshop
Bring GEO, content / SEO, analytics, and technical owners together. Agree what will be delivered, who owns it, and when evidence will be reviewed.
Delivery check-ins
Use the shared action register to remove blockers, confirm approvals, and inspect live-change evidence. Escalate unresolved dependencies.
Evidence review
Evaluate outcomes with the business owner. If evidence is inconclusive, say so; record the next move, its owner, and review date.
ScaleIterateHoldStop

Shared delivery record

Keep the work auditable

Initiative brief
Objective, scope, brand / market, tracked cohort, measurement method, accountable lead, and target review date.
Action & change register
Actions, owners, approvals, dependencies, delivery route, publish dates, and validation evidence. Log other changes that may affect the comparison.
Evidence & decision log
Baseline and review evidence, observation windows, coverage and limitations, agreed next decision, owner, and review date.

What closes the loop

A deployment is a milestone.
A decision closes the loop.

  • Work is validated
  • Outcome is evaluated; limitations are clear
  • Next decision, owner & review date are agreed

Outcome: A customer-owned first loop: validated delivery, an auditable evidence record, and an agreed next decision.
