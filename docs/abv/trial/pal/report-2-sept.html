Executive snapshot

PAL owns its home-market answer — the risk is the pipe, not the presence

Across four weeks (2026 W32–W35), Philippine Airlines is the dominant brand in AI answers for Philippine air-travel queries: #1 share of voice in all 10 tracked topics and citations that out-pace every regional competitor combined. The gap is infrastructural — a large share of what AI agents crawl never renders into readable content, and a growing set of non-branded comparison prompts is slipping. Fix the delivery layer and the visibility already earned converts into far more citations.

Content-hygiene leak

Non-production surfaces are being cited by AI engines — staging-aem.philippineairlines.com alone shows 176 citations, and beta.philippineairlines.com appears in prompt-level citation sets. Staging/beta content in the AI index dilutes canonical authority and risks surfacing unapproved or stale copy in answers.

Fix

Block non-prod domains at two layers — robots.txt Disallow plus a X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow response header on staging-aem.* and beta.* — then de-index what’s already surfaced. Using both controls is stronger than either alone.

Category · Agentic infrastructure & crawl health

Category · Prerender opportunity

Agents see ~12 words where a reader sees 500+ — this is the headline lever

PAL’s pages render content client-side, so AI agents that don’t execute JavaScript receive a near-empty shell. LLM Optimizer’s content gain ratio quantifies the gap: across 822 measured URLs, the median page exposes ~12 words before prerender and ~524 after — a 42.4× median gain (mean 77.5×, max 963×).

Why this is the priority

Every other metric in this report sits downstream of this one. The comparison-prompt erosion, the low citation conversion (1.3%), the thin content-visibility scores (2.4) are all consistent with agents reading almost none of PAL’s actual page content. Turning on prerender / Deploy on Edge for the high-traffic set is the highest-leverage action available — and LLM Optimizer’s “Recover Content Visibility” optimization is built for exactly this, auto-refreshing when page content changes.

Prioritized actions

A delivery-layer roadmap, grounded in Adobe guidance

Ordered by leverage against the data. Priorities 1–3 recover citations from content PAL already owns; 4–6 defend and extend share of voice.

Enable prerender / Deploy on Edge for the high-traffic set P1 · highest leverage

Serve prerendered, data-hydrated HTML to AI user-agents via CDN routing (two backends — SPA + prerendered — matched on bot UA in the CDN config), starting with the ~15 URLs that carry the bulk of the 291K agent hits on ≥10× pages. Use LLM Optimizer’s Recover Content Visibility optimization, which auto-refreshes on content change, and verify with Fixed Suggestions → View Live.

Grounded in Adobe KB: CDN/edge pre-rendering by user-agent; LLM Optimizer optimization types & View Live verification.

Clear the 404 / 301 / 403 crawl waste P1

Remediate the 18.1% non-200 tail: fix or 301-map the dead travel-advisory URLs returning 100% 404; collapse redirect chains behind the 23K 301s; and audit the 3,493 × 403 — compare browser vs. bot curl to confirm WAF/bot-management isn’t challenging legitimate AI agents, then allowlist known crawler UAs. Prioritize by category demand (loyalty, booking, travel-advisory first).

Grounded in Adobe KB: diagnosing AI-agent accessibility behind WAF/Cloudflare via UA comparison.

Contain the /kr and robots.txt failures P1 · quick win

The /kr homepage (0.31% success, 5,132 hits) and a robots.txt that only answers 15.4% of the time are actively costing citations and leaving agents without crawl rules. Treat both as incidents: stabilize the origin/redirect for /kr and ensure robots.txt returns a clean 200 to all agents.

De-index staging & beta subdomains P2

Stop non-production content leaking into AI answers: add Disallow rules in robots.txt for staging-aem.* and beta.*, set an X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow response header at the dispatcher/vhost for those hosts, and de-index already-surfaced URLs. Both controls together are stronger than either alone.

Grounded in Adobe KB: preventing non-prod domains from being crawled/indexed (robots.txt + X-Robots-Tag).

Win back the non-branded comparison prompts P2

Target the 36 prompts that fell to 0% and the “Compare & Choose Airlines” topic (14.6% SoV). These answers are currently sourced from aggregators and competitor route pages. Ensure owned route, fare-comparison and “best for [traveler type]” content is prerendered and answer-shaped (clear headings, structured data, direct comparison facts) so the model can pull PAL as the primary source.

Close aggregator content gaps & instrument the rest P3

Map the third-party domains filling PAL’s answer space (FlightConnections, FlightsFrom, competitor route pages) to owned pages that should outrank them, and lift the thin content-visibility scores (avg 2.4). Confirm CDN log forwarding is capturing the full request population from the outermost CDN so Agentic Traffic and Brand Presence reporting stays complete as fixes land.

Grounded in Adobe KB: LLM Optimizer CDN configuration & log forwarding in multi-CDN setups.
