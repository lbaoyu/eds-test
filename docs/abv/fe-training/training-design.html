Learning design and assessment

One case. One cumulative workbook.

Each session builds on the same business problem. Learners leave with connected decisions and artifacts, rather than isolated product exercises.

Illustrative customer scenario

A bank wants to improve its presence in AI answers about travel credit cards in a priority market.

Define the audience and objective, assess readiness, investigate competitive gaps, develop a verified content improvement, and explain the resulting evidence. Adapt the scenario to the customer's industry and market.

Delivery rhythm

Explain. Demonstrate. Apply. Debrief.

Explain the decision, demonstrate the workflow, let learners apply it, and debrief the evidence. Schedule sessions roughly one week apart for practical preparation between sessions.

Capstone assessment

Assess judgment, not feature recall.

Learners justify an opportunity, propose an appropriate optimization, and explain a defensible measurement approach. Evaluate business alignment, evidence quality, feasibility, and interpretation.

Measurement layer 1

Delivery

What was approved, changed, and deployed? Record the owner, scope, and dates.

Measurement layer 2

Visibility

What changed in relevant mentions, citations, or answer accuracy within a comparable scope?

Measurement layer 3

Business outcomes

Where observable, connect AI referrals with engagement or conversion. State tracking and attribution limits.

Define success early. Interpret results honestly.

Establish the baseline in Readiness and log interventions during Optimize. Use a prepared dataset for the final exercise: live visibility changes are not guaranteed to appear within the training window, and before/after movement alone does not establish causation.

Facilitator preparation: Prepare the cumulative workbook, a scoped demo environment, a sample prompt set, a target content example, and a clearly labeled training dataset. Confirm current product availability, report definitions, access, and implementation options before delivery. These supporting materials are separate from this course outline.
