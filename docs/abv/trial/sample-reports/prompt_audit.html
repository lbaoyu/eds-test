02 GEO goals & KPIs

Which prompt group tests which goal

The GEO Baseline and Optimization Plan frames the program around four goals for the Summit Cash Rewards Card. This audit checks whether the locked 64 prompts actually give each goal something to measure.

Prompt-group-to-goal mapping

Which prompts test which goal, and what each mapping is actually checking for.

GoalPrompt group(s)What it tests
G1 · Win unbranded discoveryUnbranded cash-back discovery and comparison (24)Whether the card surfaces without being named
G2 · Agent-readable product pagesPublic rates, fees, and eligibility (8)Whether ChatGPT can access precise public terms — ties directly to the rates-and-fees page flagged in the GEO plan
G3 · Be the cited sourceBranded & product (16) + Rewards & redemption (16)Whether Cedarline’s own page earns the citation once the card is already the topic
G4 · Convert visibility into engagementNone — out of scope for this prompt setCannot be tested by any prompt; it’s a site-traffic metric, not an AI-answer metric
G4 is a structural blind spot, not a gap in this prompt set. A prompt audit tests what AI says in response to a question — it has no way to observe what a person does after reading the answer. G4’s KPI (AI-referred sessions) needs the Adobe Analytics connector called out as Priority 4 in the GEO plan, not a new prompt. Section 08 returns to this.

Audit interpretation. The set covers four useful question types: named-product awareness, unbranded discovery, rewards use, and public product terms. Unbranded discovery is the weakest group, but it is not absent — the card is mentioned in 40% of runs and a Cedarline page is cited in 22%. That makes it a meaningful area to monitor through the planned optimization rather than a failed or irrelevant prompt group.

04 AI Visibility

The market-discovery layer behind the locked prompt set

AI Visibility is the market-discovery layer; the locked 64 prompts are the controlled measurement set. The Semrush-powered views draw on a shared AI prompt database for broad market context — useful for discovering topics and candidates, but not for combining metrics with the 64-prompt ChatGPT results. Of the three views, only Prompt Research surfaces prompt-level candidate language; Visibility Overview and Market Comparison supply market context used to validate scope, not to source prompts directly.

05 Prompt Strategy

The candidate-review stage between discovery and tracking

Prompt Strategy sits between broad discovery and the prompts Cedarline actively tracks. It surfaces suggestions from four sources; reviewed candidates are added to the Prompt Library with Track.

Source 1

Semrush suggestions

Identify market-relevant conversations about cash-back and comparison cards; use ChatGPT observations where the selection lets you choose the AI engine.

Source 2

Google Search Console

If connected, use actual Cedarline search queries as supporting evidence for customer language. Treat search queries as an input, not automatically as AI prompts.

Source 3

Synthetic Personas

Use persona-based suggestions to test whether the set covers different generic customer needs — comparing cards, understanding redemption — without introducing personalized credit advice.

Source 4

Citation Attempt

Check whether pages AI agents access as potential sources are represented by the tracked prompt set — especially relevant to the three pages flagged in the GEO baseline.

Selection rule. Keep suggestions only when they fit the Summit Cash Rewards Card, the U.S. market, ChatGPT scope, and a distinct customer question. 06 Enhanced Prompt Library

The source of truth for the 64 tracked prompts

Unlike AI Visibility’s broad discovery data, the library holds the curated prompt set used for ongoing Brand Presence measurement. Prompts appear in a flat list organized by Category, Subcategory, Market, and Language.

Health checks before relying on the set for comparison

  1. Branded / unbranded balance — confirm the classification on each prompt record; the four group labels alone don’t establish the exact branded/unbranded ratio, since rewards and terms questions could be either.
  2. Intent coverage — confirm the library covers branded awareness, unbranded discovery, rewards use, and public product terms, not just one stage of the card journey.
  3. Agentic URL coverage — confirm the three pages in the GEO baseline are represented by relevant tracked prompts; the baseline identifies page-level issues but not the library’s URL-coverage status.
  4. Market, language, and platform — confirm the U.S./English scope and keep the measured results ChatGPT-only.
  5. Prompt status and processing — confirm all 64 prompts are active and processed before the measurement window; newly added prompts must process (up to 24 hours) before contributing to Brand Presence.

Suggestions surfaced directly in the library

Beyond hosting the 64 tracked prompts, the library’s own Agentic URL Coverage panel flags pages where agent-request volume outpaces tracked-prompt coverage — a second, narrower stream of suggestions distinct from the broader Prompt Strategy queue in Section 04.

08 Audit conclusion

Fit for the defined baseline; prompt-by-prompt checks remain open

The 64-prompt set is fit for the defined ChatGPT-only baseline at the group level. It sits within the requested size range, covers four distinct credit-card question types, and makes the unbranded discovery gap visible without suggesting Cedarline is absent from all answers.

Confirmed at the group level

What this audit confirms

  • 64 unique prompts, within the intended 50–80 range
  • Four distinct question types, each with a clear measurement role
  • Clean mapping to three of the four GEO goals (G1, G2, G3)
  • Distinct, non-overlapping roles for AI Visibility, Prompt Strategy, and the Enhanced Prompt Library
  • A page-to-prompt-group coverage map for the three Optimize at Edge pages

Needs the raw prompt rows — or a different instrument

What still needs verification

  • Individual prompt wording, and duplicate or near-duplicate prompts
  • Branded/unbranded tags on each prompt record
  • Actual Agentic URL Coverage status for the three priority pages
  • AI Visibility and Prompt Strategy export settings (market, filter, date reviewed)
  • G4 — no prompt can test it; it needs the Adobe Analytics connector, not this audit

Scope & honesty notes

This audit uses the group-level composition and results published in the companion GEO Baseline and Optimization Plan; it does not have the raw 64 prompt rows or the AI Visibility and Prompt Strategy exports, so prompt-by-prompt quality checks (wording, duplicates, tags) cannot be completed from the report alone. Sharing those rows or exports would let this become a prompt-by-prompt audit. Cedarline Bank, the Summit Cash Rewards Credit Card, and all figures remain fictional and illustrative.
