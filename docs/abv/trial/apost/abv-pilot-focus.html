Start with the business decision

Lead with MyPost Business domestic shipping

The strongest starting candidate is small-business shipping consideration: helping Australian online retailers discover and evaluate MyPost Business for domestic parcel sending.

Proposed scope in one sentence

Improve Australia Post's accurate visibility in AI answers when small Australian online retailers ask how to choose a domestic shipping service, reduce shipping costs, and connect their store to a sending workflow.

Why this is a strong starting point

A clear commercial journey

The audience has a concrete decision to make, Australia Post has a defined product to recommend, and owned pages already explain its savings, sending tools, and integrations. The relevant GEO outcomes are discovery, citations, and correct service representation. [1]

Why the scope is actionable

Specific content within Australia Post's control

Product and guidance pages can clarify savings conditions, suitability, integrations, and pickup limits. Cross-page spend wording also warrants owner review: the main page says “$50+ each week,” while the NZ page says “$50+ over four weeks.” This is a consistency question, not proof of an AI-answer error. What each choice puts inside the pilot

Same structure, different questions and KPIs

Read each brief as a chain: audience need → content scope → prompt themes → GEO outcome. The prompts are illustrative seeds, not a final library or evidence of search popularity.

A · Product consideration

MyPost Business domestic shipping

Customer question: “Which parcel shipping solution fits my small Australian online store?”

Audience & journey

Low-volume Australian online retailers evaluating domestic shipping, savings, and store integration before choosing a sending solution.

Include

Product suitability, savings-band conditions, domestic sending tools, Shopify/eBay integration, and pickup eligibility. Focus on the MyPost Business page and closely related guidance. [1]

Content opportunity to investigate

A clearer small-merchant explanation of who the service suits, how savings work, and which limits apply. Reconcile conflicting spend wording before treating it as an approved fact.

Comparison context

Relevant domestic carrier alternatives appearing in the baseline; Aramex and CouriersPlease are initial candidates to validate. Keep shipping-software partners separate from carriers.

Illustrative prompt seeds

Unbranded · DiscoveryWhat parcel delivery options should a small Australian online store compare if it sends around 10 orders a week?

Unbranded · EvaluationHow can a small Australian eCommerce business reduce domestic shipping costs without signing a large-volume contract?

Branded · AccuracyHow do Australia Post MyPost Business savings bands work, and which spending counts toward them?

GEO KPI focus

Primary: unbranded brand-mention rate—how often Australia Post appears as a relevant option when the question does not name it.

Supporting: owned-domain citation rate; accuracy of savings and pickup claims; competitive share of voice.

Exclude: international shipping, enterprise contracts, returns as a separate journey, and customer-specific parcel tracking. Do not imply maximum savings or pickup availability applies to every merchant.

B · Service adoption & clarity

Domestic returns for small retailers

Customer question: “How can I offer convenient domestic returns, and what do my customers need to do?”

Audience & journey

Australian small retailers choosing or explaining a MyPost Business returns process. Customer drop-off questions support the merchant journey rather than creating a separate consumer-support scope.

Include

Domestic eligibility, label creation, postage responsibilities, printer-less returns, and supported drop-off steps. Focus on parcel returns guidance and MyPost Business conditions. [1] [2]

Content opportunity to investigate

A clearer explanation of label printing versus postage charges, service exclusions, and the difference between Australia Post's return process and the retailer's refund policy.

Comparison context

Carrier guidance, shipping-platform help centers, and retailer FAQs actually cited by AI answers. Not every competing source is a competing carrier.

Illustrative prompt seeds

Unbranded · DiscoveryWhat printer-less parcel return options can an Australian online shop offer its customers?

Branded · SetupHow do I create a domestic return label for a customer using Australia Post MyPost Business?

Branded · AccuracyIs Australia Post Print at Post free return shipping, or does it only cover printing the return label?

GEO KPI focus

Primary: owned-domain citation rate—how often relevant returns answers cite Australia Post's own guidance.

Supporting: accuracy of fees and eligibility; unbranded brand-mention rate. Keep branded and unbranded results distinct.

Exclude: international returns, retailer refund-policy decisions, and a full eParcel returns comparison. Free label printing does not mean free return postage; Print at Post is available at select outlets.

C · Export enablement

First-time business shipping to New Zealand

Customer question: “How do I send my first customer orders from Australia to New Zealand?”

Audience & journey

Australian small retailers evaluating and preparing a first cross-border shipment. Australia remains the monitored market; New Zealand is the destination.

Include

Service choice, MyPost Business label/customs support, documentation, destination restrictions, and postage-estimate guidance. Focus on the NZ destination page and linked international guidance. [3] [4]

Content opportunity to investigate

An answer-first destination guide that connects service options, a first-shipment checklist, and current customs information without forcing merchants to assemble the journey across multiple pages.

Comparison context

Relevant NZ shipping alternatives such as DHL Express and FedEx, subject to service validation. Treat customs-authority sources separately from carriers.

Illustrative prompt seeds

Unbranded · DiscoveryHow can a small Australian online store start shipping customer orders to New Zealand?

Unbranded · PreparationWhat customs information does an Australian business need when sending merchandise to New Zealand?

Branded · EvaluationHow do Australia Post International Standard and International Express differ for business parcels to New Zealand?

GEO KPI focus

Primary: owned-domain citation rate for NZ shipping questions.

Supporting: unbranded brand-mention rate; accuracy of service and customs explanations.

Exclude: other destinations, global customs advice, and universal price/delivery guarantees. Current rules need product/compliance ownership; do not hard-code assumed duty exemptions.

D · Research authority

Australian eCommerce insights for merchant planning

Customer question: “Which Australian shopping trends should inform my online store's plans?”

Audience & journey

Australian retailers researching market trends and shopper behavior before making planning decisions. Australia Post is the research source, not necessarily the recommended shipping provider.

Include

The 2026 eCommerce report, its official PDF, and a tightly defined set of merchant-relevant findings and summaries. [5]

Content opportunity to investigate

Accessible HTML summaries with clear statistic attribution, data years, and methodology. The rendered landing page exposes limited report detail, so test retrieval before assuming there is an accessibility failure.

Comparison context

Other credible Australian retail and consumer-research sources cited in answers—not carrier share of voice.

Illustrative prompt seeds

Unbranded · ResearchWhat are the latest Australian online shopping trends that a small retailer should use for planning?

Unbranded · Source selectionWhere can I find reliable data on Australian eCommerce spending and shopper behavior?

Branded · AccuracyWhat are the key findings of the Australia Post eCommerce Report 2026, and which period does its data cover?

GEO KPI focus

Primary: report-family citation rate—how often answers cite the selected official report, PDF, or summary URLs.

Supporting: correct Australia Post research attribution; accuracy of statistics and data-year labels.

Exclude: optimizing the on-site AI assistant, all research publications, and parcel-sales uplift as the primary GEO outcome. Validate every finding against the report before including it in approved content.

Turn the shortlist into a bounded scope

Agree one journey and its GEO outcome

Select the candidate that combines a business priority, a real AI-answer gap, and owned content the team can change. If MyPost Business already has strong visibility with little headroom, choose a better evidenced alternative rather than assume acquisition is automatically the best test.

Keep the scope focused

Returns is the closest adjacent option, but it should not enter the first scope by default. Add a second journey only if it serves the same agreed business objective without blurring prompt ownership or the primary KPI.

Prompt volume, model coverage, numerical targets, and measurement design follow the scope decision. They are intentionally outside this document. Customer priorities, actual answer gaps, and capability availability remain to be confirmed.
