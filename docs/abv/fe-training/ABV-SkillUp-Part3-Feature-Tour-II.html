Strategy type

Evolving

A manually managed initiative with one shared objective and related work over time. Can group multiple opportunities.

Measure: baseline when the strategy is created; report on the strategy scope.

Strategy type

Atomic

A supported deployment or fix where a specific action or batch needs measurement. Typically tied to one deployment.

Parent and child records. The parent strategy holds objective, scope, baseline, follow-up, and interpretation. Child rows document actions, owners, and dates — not individual performance deltas.

Synthesis · close the loop

Make a defensible next decision

Decision

Scale

The agreed criteria are met; repeat or expand with a clearly defined next scope.

Decision

Iterate

Refine the action or approach while keeping the original comparison identifiable.

Decision

Hold

Evidence is insufficient; assign the blocker and next review date. Measurement stays open.

Decision

Stop

Continuing is not justified; record the learning and any correction or rollback.

A deployment or UI status alone is not completion. A well-reasoned iterate or hold is a successful outcome — the purpose is a defensible decision, not a forced positive result.
